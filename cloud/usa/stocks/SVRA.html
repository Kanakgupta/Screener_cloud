Company Overview

Savara Inc., a clinical stage biopharmaceutical company, focuses on rare respiratory diseases in the United States. The company's lead product candidate is molgramostim, an inhaled granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor, which is in Phase 3 development stage for the treatment of autoimmune pulmonary alveolar proteinosis. Savara Inc. was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Yardley, Pennsylvania.
