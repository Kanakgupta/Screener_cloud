Company Overview

Borealis Foods Inc. provides nutritious and ready-to-eat food products in the United States, Canada, and internationally. It provides ramen noodles under the Chef Woo, Chef Ramsay, Ramen Express, and Woodles brands. The company sells its products through retail stores, merchandisers, club stores, and supermarkets, as well as e-commerce distributors. Borealis Foods Inc. is based in Oakville, Canada.
