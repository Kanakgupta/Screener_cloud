Company Overview

Robo.ai Inc. operates as a technology company. The company focuses on building infrastructure across artificial intelligence; robotics and smart mobility; advanced manufacturing; and digital assets and capital. It engages in the development, integration, and manufacturing of AI systems for government, public sector, and mission-critical domains; and development of AI software and visual data infrastructure. The company was formerly known as NWTN, Inc. and changed its name to Robo.ai Inc. in August 2025. Robo.ai Inc. is headquartered in Dubai, the United Arab Emirates.
