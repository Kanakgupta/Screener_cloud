Company Overview

Invitation Homes Inc., an S&P 500 company, is the nation's premier single-family home leasing and management company, helping to expand housing through new development and strategic partnerships. Their purpose, Unlock the Power of Home, reflects their commitment to address America's housing needs by delivering high-quality living solutions and genuine care to those who choose the flexibility and value of leasing. Invitation Homes Inc. was incorporated in 6th June 2012 and is based in Dallas, United States.
