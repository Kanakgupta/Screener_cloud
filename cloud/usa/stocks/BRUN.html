Company Overview

Boost Run, Inc. provides artificial intelligence cloud infrastructure and high-performance computing solutions. The company offers a range of graphics processing unit (GPU) servers hosted within datacenter facilities. Its platform provides GPU compute, CPU nodes, manage Kubernetes orchestration, and shared storage. The company was founded in 2023 and is based in Northbrook, Illinois.
