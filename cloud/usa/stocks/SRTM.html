Company Overview

Stratum Holdings Corp. operates as an AI infrastructure company that focuses on GPU licensing and scalable compute infrastructure. It offers enterprise-grade GPU farms to accelerate computational workloads, AI development, and digital asset management. The company also operates its residential and commercial real estate brokerage, franchising, education and coaching, and property management businesses. Stratum Holdings Corp. was formerly known as La Rosa Holdings Corp. and changed its name to Stratum Holdings Corp. in October2026. The company was founded in 2004 and is based in Celebration, Florida.
