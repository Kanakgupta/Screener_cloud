Company Overview

SkyAI, Inc. engages in building an Agentic finance for the global south. It combines stablecoin rails with AI to deliver financial access, education, and actionable intelligence to the underbanked users in Asia, Latin America, and Africa. The company was formerly known as Sharps Technology, Inc. and changed its name to SkyAI, Inc. in May 2026. SkyAI, Inc. was incorporated in 2017 and is based in Melville, New York.
