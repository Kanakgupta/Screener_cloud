Company Overview

U.S. Gold Corp. engages in the exploration, evaluation, and development of mineral properties in the United States. It explores for gold, silver, copper, and precious metals deposits. The company's flagship property is its 100% owned CK gold project, which consists of various mining leases and other mineral rights covering an area of approximately 1,120 acres located in Laramie County, Wyoming. U.S. Gold Corp. was founded in 2014 and is based in Elko, Nevada.
