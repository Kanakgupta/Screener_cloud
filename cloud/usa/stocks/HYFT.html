Company Overview

MindWalk Holdings Corp. operates as a bio-native AI company. The company discovers and develops biologics through HYFT Technology and LensAI platform, as well as develops AI drug discovery and biologics through ReefIQ technology. The company was formerly known as ImmunoPrecise Antibodies Ltd. and changed its name to MindWalk Holdings Corp. in September 2025. The company was incorporated in 1983 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas.
