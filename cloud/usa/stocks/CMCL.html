Company Overview

Caledonia Mining Corporation Plc engages in the exploration, development, and production of gold in Zimbabwe. The company is also involved in the exploration and development of mineral properties for precious metals. It owns a 64% stake in the gold-producing Blanket Mine, 100% stake in the Bilboes Sulphide Project, and the Motapa and Maligreen gold mining claims in Zimbabwe. The company was incorporated in 1992 and is headquartered in Saint Helier, Jersey.
