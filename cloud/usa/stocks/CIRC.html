Company Overview

Circle8 Group, Inc. provides talent management services. The company offers services, such as finding and engaging talent for various employment contracts, including secondment, interim positions, and temp-to-perm roles. The company serves the IT, technology, and finance industries. The company was formerly known as Atlantic International Corp. and changed its name to Circle8 Group, Inc. in June 2026. Circle8 Group, Inc. is headquartered in Englewood Cliffs, New Jersey.
