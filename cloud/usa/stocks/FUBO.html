Company Overview

FuboTV Inc. operates as a live TV streaming platform company in the United States, Canada, France, Spain, and internationally. The company offers sports, news, and entertainment program through Fubo-branded and Hulu live-branded services. FuboTV Inc. is headquartered in New York, New York. FuboTV Inc. operates as a subsidiary of The Walt Disney Company.
