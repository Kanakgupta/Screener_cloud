Company Overview

WhiteHawk Minerals Corp. focuses on natural gas mineral and royalty business in the United States. Its assets are primarily in the Marcellus and Haynesville Shales, which are located in the Appalachian and Haynesville Basins. The company was formerly known as WhiteHawk Income Corporation and changed its name to WhiteHawk Minerals Corp. in June 2026. The company was incorporated in 2022 and is based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
