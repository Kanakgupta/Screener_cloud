Company Overview

Uranium Royalty Corp. operates as a uranium and land royalty company in Canada. It engages in acquiring and assembling a portfolio of royalties; investing in companies with exposure to uranium and physical uranium; and the purchase and sale of physical uranium. The company was incorporated in 2017 and is based in Lakewood, Colorado.
