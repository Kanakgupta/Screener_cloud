Modine Manufacturing Company designs, engineers, tests, manufactures, and sells mission-critical thermal solutions in the United States, Canada, Italy, Hungary, the United Kingdom, China, and internationally. The compny was incorporated in 1916 and is headquartered in Racine, Wisconsin.
