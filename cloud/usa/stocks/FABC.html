Company Overview

Fabric.AI, Inc. designs and develops fabless semiconductor technologies. The company provides MicroLED based optical interconnect, as well as suite of fabless semiconductor technologies designed for AI workloads. It also engages in the development and commercialization of interface for GPU-to-GPU connectivity to develop a prototype and demonstration version of the project technology. Fabric.AI, Inc. has a strategic partnership with Kopin Corporation to develop Neural I/o MicroLED optical interconnect technology for AI Data Centers. The company was formerly known as StableX Technologies, Inc. and changed its name to Fabric.AI, Inc. in April 2026. The company was founded in 2017 and is based in New York, New York.
