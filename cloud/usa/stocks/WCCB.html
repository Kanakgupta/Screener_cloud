Company Overview

West Coast Community Bancorp operates as the bank holding company for West Coast Community Bank that provides a range of commercial and personal banking services to residents and businesses in the United States. The company offers personal and business checking, savings, money market, health savings, and individual retirement accounts, as well as certificate of deposits; and retirement plans. It also provides business, real estate commercial property and construction, and asset-based lending; agricultural and wine industry lending products; small business administration, 504 loan program, business and industry loans, and farm services agency loans; and lines of credit. In addition, the company offers merchant services, bill payment, treasury management, remote deposit capture, debit and credit cards, merchant gift cards, courier services, lockbox, cash vault, safe deposits box, ATMs and online and mobile banking services. West Coast Community Bancorp was founded in 2004 and is headquartered in Santa Cruz, California.
