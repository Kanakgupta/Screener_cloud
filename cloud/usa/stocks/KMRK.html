Company Overview

K-TECH Solutions Company Limited, through its subsidiary, designs, develops, tests, and sells various toy products in the United Kingdom, Europe, and the United States. The company offers infant, pre-school educational, plastic, and electromechanical toys. It also provides learning kits. The company was founded in 2016 and is based in Kwai Chung, Hong Kong.
