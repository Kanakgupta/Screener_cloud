Company Overview

Faraday Future Intelligent Electric Inc. is a U.S.-based Physical AI ecosystem company that offers robotics and mobility solutions through AI innovation and technologies. The company focuses in two product strategies within the Embodied AI (EAI) robotics business; EAI humanoid and bionic robots, and EAI automotive-focused robots. The company was formerly known as Faraday&Future Inc. and changed its name to Faraday Future Intelligent Electric Inc. in July 2022. Faraday Future Intelligent Electric Inc. was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in El Segundo, California.
