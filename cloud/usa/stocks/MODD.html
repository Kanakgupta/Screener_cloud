Company Overview

Modular Medical, Inc. operates as a medical device company. The company focuses on the design, development, and commercialization of insulin pumps. It offers Pivot product, a tubeless insulin delivery system that integrates the set into a true tubeless patch. Modular Medical, Inc. is headquartered in San Diego, California.
