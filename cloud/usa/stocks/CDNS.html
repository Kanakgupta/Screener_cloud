Cadence provides AI-driven electronic-design-automation software and IP used to design the chips and systems that power AI — and increasingly uses AI within its own tools.

Core Focus

Computational-software for chip design, verification and system analysis, with AI woven throughout (Verisium, Cerebrus) and a growing IP business.

Key Initiatives
  • AI-driven design (Cerebrus/Verisium) Cadence embeds generative and reinforcement-learning AI to automate and optimise chip design and verification.
