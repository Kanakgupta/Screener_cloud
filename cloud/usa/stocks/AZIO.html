Company Overview

Azio AI Holdings, Inc. manufactures and provides zero-emission electric vehicles in the United States. It offers class 2 through logistics vans; class 4 through urban trucks, school buses, electric forklifts, street sweepers, neighborhood electric vehicles, and right-hand drive vans and urban trucks. The company also provides vehicle maintenance and safety inspection services; and AI infrastructure hosting services. The company serves commercial and last-mile fleets, school districts, public and private transportation service companies, and colleges and universities. Azio AI Holdings, Inc. was formerly known as Envirotech Vehicles, Inc. and changed its name to Azio AI Holdings, Inc. in July 2026. The company is headquartered in Houston, Texas.
