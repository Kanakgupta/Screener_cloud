Company Overview

PLAYSTUDIOS, Inc. develops and publishes free-to-play casual games for mobile and social platforms in the United States and internationally. The company operates in two segments, playGAMES and playAWARDS. Its game portfolio includes a diverse range of titles, such as social casino, card, and puzzle games under the myVEGAS Slots, myVEGAS Blackjack, my KONAMI Slots, POP! Slots, myVEGAS Bingo, MGM Slots Live, Solitaire, Jumbline 2, Spider Solitaire, Sudoku, and Mahjong names. The company also offers loyalty programs for the games industry. The company was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada.
