What are the biggest financial risks for Bank & Finance companies?

What happens to shareholders if a company becomes insolvent?

Insured U.S. banks cannot file under Title 11 of the U.S. Code; a failing bank is closed and placed into FDIC receivership. Deposits are insured up to $250,000, but the bank holding company's shareholders are usually wiped out (as with SVB Financial Group in 2023).
