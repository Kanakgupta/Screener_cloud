How to trade this screen

What it means

Stocks up > 3% on the latest session, volume ≥ 1.5× avg, price above both 50DMA and 200DMA — multi-factor momentum confirmation. The Setup column flags any concurrent breakout, support bounce, or bullish reversal candle.

When to use it

Highest-probability entries when the Setup tag shows a breakout or support bounce and sector strength is broad. Skip names that gapped > 5% (entry risk too high).

How to act

BUY on intraday pullback toward the day’s VWAP / opening range, or on next-day confirmation hold. Stop: below the signal-bar low. Target: 2–3× initial risk.

Risks & caveats

Heavy-volume up-days can mark exhaustion tops if RSI is already > 75 and the stock is > 20% above its 50DMA. Always size to predefined R-risk.

Educational guidance only — not investment advice. Always size positions to your own risk tolerance and use a stop-loss.
