Technical Pattern Screener

Scans the daily chart of 2147 stocks for chart and candlestick patterns triggered in the last 2 trading days. Pick a pattern from the sidebar to see all stocks where it just fired, with the exact trigger date.

How signals are filtered & scored
Each detected pattern is graded 0–100 based on:
  • Volume on the trigger bar vs 20-day average (≥1.5× boosts score, <1× penalises breakouts)
  • Trend alignment — close vs 50-EMA / 200-EMA and EMA50 slope
  • ATR sanity — body/range relative to 14-day ATR (filters tiny doji noise)
  • Weekly trend via 10-EMA of weekly closes (multi-timeframe)
  • ADX(14) > 25 trending bonus, <15 choppy penalty for breakouts
Stocks with 20-day avg traded value below the regional liquidity floor are excluded. Breakout patterns also require trigger-bar volume ≥ 1.1× the 20-day average — a price poke without volume is dropped, not just down-scored. Only signals with score ≥ 55 are listed; rows are sorted by score (high to low). Hover the score badge to see the exact factors that contributed.
