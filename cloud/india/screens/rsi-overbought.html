How to trade this screen

What it means

14-day RSI above 80 — extreme overbought; price has run too far too fast.

When to use it

In a strong uptrend, treat overbought as strength, not a sell signal. In a sideways or rolling-over market, it’s a profit-taking cue.

How to act

TRIM longs / book partial profits. Aggressive: SHORT on a bearish reversal candle with stop above the recent high. Avoid pure shorting in strong uptrends.

Risks & caveats

Overbought can persist for many sessions in trending moves. Wait for momentum divergence or a lower-high before shorting.
