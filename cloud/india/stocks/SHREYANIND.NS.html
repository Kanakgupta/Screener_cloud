Company Overview

Shreyans Industries Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of writing and printing papers and soda ash in India and internationally. The company offers high-brightness, cream wove, coloured, duplicating, surface-sized printing, azure laid, maplitho, stamp, inland letter, postal envelope, offset, cover, super calendered, rail ticket, and super printing paper products. It serves copy manufacturers, job printers, various states of textbook depots/boards, exporters of notebooks and diaries, printing and stationary departments, railways, P and T departments, and security presses. The company was formerly known as Shreyans Paper Mills Ltd. and changed its name to Shreyans Industries Limited in October 1992. Shreyans Industries Limited was incorporated in 1979 and is based in Ludhiana, India.
