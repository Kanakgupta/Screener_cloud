Company Overview

Global Surfaces Limited engages in the mining, production, and export of natural stones and engineered quartz in the United States, the United Arab Emirates, and India. It offers natural stones, such as granites, marbles, soft quartzites, and phylites and soapstones; and engineered quartz products, including stratum, prismatic, quartzites, kalmasa, and aurora. The company also trades in quartz slabs. Global Surfaces Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is headquartered in Jaipur, India.
