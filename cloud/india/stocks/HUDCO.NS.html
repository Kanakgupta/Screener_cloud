Company Overview

Housing and Urban Development Corporation Limited provides financing and credit for housing and infrastructure development projects in India. The company offers housing finance loans for social housing, residential real estate, and HUDCO Niwas; urban infrastructure finance loans for water supply, roads and transport, power, emerging sector, commercial infrastructure and others, social infrastructure and area development, and sewerage and drainage; and take-out finance loans. It also provides consultancy services, including government program services; and advisory on urban and regional planning, design and development, environmental engineering, and social development. The company was incorporated in 1970 and is headquartered in New Delhi, India.
