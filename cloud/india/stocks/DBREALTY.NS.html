Company Overview

Valor Estate Limited, together with its subsidiaries, operates as a real estate construction and development company in India. It operates in two segments: Real Estate Business and Hospitality Business. The company develops and sells residential, commercial, retail, and other projects, such as mass housing and cluster redevelopment, as well as hospitality projects. The company was formerly known as D B Realty Limited and changed its name to Valor Estate Limited in March 2024. Valor Estate Limited was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Mumbai, India.
