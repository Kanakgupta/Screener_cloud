Company Overview

Sasken Technologies Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides product engineering and digital transformation services in India, North America, Europe, the Middles East, and the Asia Pacific. It operates in two segments, Software Services and Product Solutions. The company offers hardware design and development, platform and application software, and modem and embedded software development; application development, device and platform testing, and android services; ADAS and autonomous, body electronics and autosar, integrated cockpit, and telematics, V2X, and smart mobility; and off-highway machines, connected manufacturing, machine tools, and enterprise devices. It also provides mobility solutions; terminal design and development, as well as access network enhancement, maintenance, and IP; radio access networks; and fare collection and revenue management, mobility as a service, real-time passenger information, planning and dispatch, asset management, and passenger safety services. In addition, the company offers digital engineering services, including platform development and sustenance, mobility, data analytics, Internet of Things, digital testing, DevOps, blockchain, cybersecurity services, and AWS. It serves automotive, semiconductor, industrials, communication networks and devices, satellite communications, and transportation industries. The company was formerly known as Sasken Communication Technologies Limited and changed its name to Sasken Technologies Limited in February 2017. Sasken Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1989 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
