Company Overview

Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited engages in the manufacturing, sale, and marketing of white goods in India. It offers split air conditioners; LED lighting; water dispensers; commercial freezers; washing machines; compressors; and EMS-circuit boards through online platforms. The company also provides plastic molding, sheet metal, powder coating, copper tubing systems, heat exchangers, EMS manufacturing, and wire harnessing services. In addition, it exports its products. The company serves original equipment manufacturers and original design manufacturers. Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited was incorporated in 2015 and is based in Nashik, India.
