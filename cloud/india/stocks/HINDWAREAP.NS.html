Company Overview

Hindware Home Innovation Limited engages in the manufacturing, selling and trading of building products and consumer products in India. The company offers kitchen appliances comprising chimneys, built-in hobs, dishwashers, cooktops, built-in ovens, cooking range, water purifiers, and sinks; air coolers and ceiling fans; and pedestal fans, kitchen& furniture fittings, water heaters, and room heaters. It also provides building products consisting of sanitary ware, including water closets, wash basins, squatting pans, and urinals; faucets, such as bathroom, kitchen, and sensor faucets, diverters, showers, and thermostats; wellness products comprising bath-tub, shower enclosures, massage tubs, and steam generators; glazed vitrified, ceramic, vitrified, quartz, and adhesives tiles; and allied products, such as shower seats, concealed cisterns, seat covers, PVC cistern, and bidet attachments. In addition, the company provides plastic pipes and fittings, which include CPVC, UPVC, PVC, and SWR; overhead water storage tanks; and and bath fittings. It offers its products under Hindware, Hindware Italian Collection, Queo, Truflo by Hindware, and Hindware Smart Appliances brand names. The company was formerly known as Somany Home Innovation Limited and changed its name to Hindware Home Innovation Limited in May 2022. Hindware Home Innovation Limited was incorporated in 2017 and is based in Gurugram, India.
