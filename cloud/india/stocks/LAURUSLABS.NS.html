Company Overview

Laurus Labs Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells medicines and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) in India and internationally. The company offers Generics APIs and solid oral drug products for intermediates for anti-retroviral (ARV), anti-diabetic, cardiovascular, anti-asthma, ophthalmology, oncology, and gastroenterology. It also provides biotechnology services that develops and manufactures animal-origin-free recombinant proteins and cell-culture media supplements and research and development services, including drug substance development, drug product development, and biologics development. In addition, the company also offers non-human ingredients; rare disease therapeutics; and finished dosages. Laurus Labs Limited was incorporated in 2005 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
