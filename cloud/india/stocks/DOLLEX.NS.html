Company Overview

Dollex Agrotech Limited manufactures and trades in sugar and its by-products in India. It operates through Trading and Manufacturing segments. The company offers jaggery and Khandsari sugar; and by-products, such as molasses, pressmud, and bagasse. Dollex Agrotech Limited was incorporated in 2013 and is based in Datia, India.
