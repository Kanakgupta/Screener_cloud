Company Overview

Haldyn Glass Limited engages in the manufactures and sells glass bottles and containers in India and internationally. It offers glass vials of various shapes and sizes for packaging injectables, eye drops, ear drops, and other lifesaving drugs for the pharmaceutical industry, as well as clear bottles for packaging in the liquor manufacturing, cosmetics, and food and beverages industries. The company was formerly known as Haldyn Glass Gujarat Limited and changed its name to Haldyn Glass Limited in November 2011. The company was incorporated in 1991 and is based in Mumbai, India. Haldyn Glass Limited operates as a subsidiary of Haldyn Corporation Ltd.
