Company Overview

Hisar Metal Industries Limited manufactures and sells cold rolled precision stainless steel strips, stainless steel welded tubes and pipes, and stainless steel plumbing pipes and fittings in India. It also exports its products. Hisar Metal Industries Limited was incorporated in 1990 and is based in Hisar, India.
