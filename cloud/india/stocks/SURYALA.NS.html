Company Overview

Suryalata Spinning Mills Limited manufactures and sells synthetic yarns in India and internationally. It offers polyester and viscose yarns, melange, P/V blended yarns, value added yarns for suitings, shirtings, and knitting. The company was incorporated in 1983 and is based in Secunderabad, India.
