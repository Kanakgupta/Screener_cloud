Company Overview

Permanent Magnets Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of cast alloy permanent magnets, parts, and accessories of electricity and electric vehicle worldwide. The company offers automotive shunt resistors, shunt assembly, and overmolded shunt assemblies; magnetic filter, self-cleaning magnetic grill pneumatic and filter, magnetic drum separator and plate, clean magnetic grill, magnetic drawer type grill, magnetic rod and hump, magnetic rota grid separator, plate type magnetic chute, IFR machine tea filtration, drawer clean magnetic grill, single and two pole suspended permanent magnet, cross belt and magnetic roller separator, magnetic pulley, and pneumatic chute magnet; soft magnetic, special, medical grade, high performance steel, and rare earth alloys; nanocrystalline cores; energy meter core comprising current transformer, high performance nanocrystalline low and high tension current transformer cores, residual current circuit breaker, ground fault circuit interrupter, core balance transformers, common mode choke, and inverter nano cores. It also provides dowel pins, limiters, plugs, brass machined, and aluminium machined parts; die casting; PCB mount hall effect sensors; standalone plastic, and insert electrical molded parts; steel grades; alnico magnets; radial flux stator, stator laminations, and axial stator cores; CBCT and ELCB relay assemblies, and auxiliary relay; flux shields; laminated flux concentrators, U-shield, and concentrators; wound cut core; UCR and C-Shape flux concentrator; and torque and angle sensors. In addition, the company offers stamped heat sinks; power distribution components; probes; meters; and kits. Further, it provides die cast, plastic, brass, bi-metal, stainless steel and special copper alloy, and nickel alloy parts. The company serves automotive, aerospace, defense, energy and gas meters, renewable energy, medical, food beverage, and oil and gas industries. The company was incorporated in 1960 and is based in Thane, India.
