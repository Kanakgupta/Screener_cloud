Company Overview

Indiabulls Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the digital platform business in India. The company retails a range of products, such as apparel, footwear, accessories, home decor items, and other products through Yaarii, a social commerce app for reselling products. It also provides web services; real estate project advisory; construction and development services for real estate projects; and other related services. The company was formerly known as Yaari Digital Integrated Services Limited and changed its name to Indiabulls Limited in October 2025. Indiabulls Limited was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Mumbai, India.
