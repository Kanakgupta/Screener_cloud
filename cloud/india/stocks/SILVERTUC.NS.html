Company Overview

Silver Touch Technologies Limited provides information technology solutions in India, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, and France. The company offers enterprise software services, including digital engineering, enterprise architecture, cloud applications development, mobile applications, and enterprise DevOps; and managed security services, such as SOC-as-a-service, cloud security, email security and protection, VAPT service, endpoint security, website and app security, security awareness training, identity and access management, and managed detection and response services. It also provides applied AI, data analytics, blockchain and IoT, and robotic process automation; modern workplace services comprising data center, communication, and IT consulting services, as well as backup and disaster recovery and call center/IP telephony; and SAP business one, SAP S/4 HANA, SAP successfactors, hosted cloud solution for SAP, and Odoo ERP. In addition, the company offers virtual desktops on cloud, cloud monitoring on cloud, cloud monitoring and management, sliver cloud, and AWS services, as well as public, private, and hybrid cloud; and capital markets technology consulting and advisory. Further, it provides e-governance solutions, including platforms development, single window solution and dashboards, manpower services, and AI powered driving skill assessment solution, as well as G2C, G2B, and G2G portal. The company was formerly known as Silver Touch Computers Limited and changed its name to Silver Touch Technologies Limited in October 2004. Silver Touch Technologies Limited was founded in 1992 and is headquartered in Ahmedabad, India.
