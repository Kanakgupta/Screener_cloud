Company Overview

APL Apollo Tubes Limited manufactures and sells structural steel tubes in India. The company offers structural tubes for construction, automotive, machinery, furniture, etc. applications; galvanized iron products for industrial and agricultural applications; tricoat pipes, designer pipes, and door frames; black round tubes used in plumbing systems of buildings; pre galvanized sections of square, rectangular, and circular tubes used for roofing structure; and ready-made and color coated chaukhat, steel building, and fencing solutions. It also exports its products. The company was formerly known as Bihar Tubes Limited and changed its name to APL Apollo Tubes Limited in 2010. APL Apollo Tubes Limited was incorporated in 1986 and is headquartered in Noida, India.
