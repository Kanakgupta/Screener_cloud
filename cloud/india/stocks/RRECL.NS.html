Company Overview

RDB Real Estate Constructions Limited engages in the real estate business. The company develops residential projects, including integrated townships and group housing projects. Its commercial projects include the building, sale, and leasing of office spaces, malls, and shops. The company was formerly known as Baron Real Estate Limited and changed its name to RDB Real Estate Constructions Limited in January 2019. RDB Real Estate Constructions Limited was incorporated in 2018 and is headquartered in Kolkata, India.
