Company Overview

Bajaj Steel Industries Limited manufactures and sells cotton ginning and pressing machinery in India. The company offers cotton ginning and pressing machineries, including double roller ginning machines, auto feeders, cotton baling presses, pre-cleaners, lint cleaners, cotton boll openers, air separators, battery condensers, stone remover, seed cotton conveying systems, stone catchers/rock traps, air locks, belt conveyor systems, lint conveying systems, fire detectors/diverters, distribution screw conveyors, pneumatic conveying systems, bucket elevators, fully automatic ginning plant, twin auto regulators, surge hoppers, and overflow hoppers; baling presses for other applications; tractor attachments for cotton ginning industry; delinting and decorticating machinery; components and machined parts, including spares, manifold blocks, gear blanks, windmill shafts, turbine housings, gear box assemblies, head spigots, balancing masses, hand granades, oil fan assemblies, automotive hub, winder cam, and other machined components; mechanical conveyors and elevators; and dust/waste handling systems. It also provides hot air humidification systems, cold moisturizing systems, and lint slides; industrial trusses and pipelines; power transmission products; truck loaders/unloaders, telescopic conveyors, and z-elevators; electrical panels and accessories, such as power distribution board, field panel, panel, auto main failure, control, and PCC, APFC, and MCC panels; chimneys, hydraulic power packs and cylinders, structures for machineries and refineries, gantry, tanks, and silos and other storage equipment; steel buildings; and pre-fabricated house. In addition, the company offers fire alarm and detection systems, fire extinguishers, and fire hydrant pumps; steel doors; laser cutting machines; and trades cotton products. The company was incorporated in 1961 and is headquartered in Nagpur, India.
