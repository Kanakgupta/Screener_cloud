Company Overview

DCM Shriram Fine Chemicals Limited engages in the manufacturing and selling of chemical products and intermediates in India and internationally. Its Pharma products include D (-) alpha phenyl glycine base, D (-) alpha phenyl glycine ethyl potassium dane salt, D (-) alpha phenyl glycine chloride hydrochloride, D (-) para hydroxy phenyl glycine base, D (-) para hydroxy phenyl glycine methyl potassium dane salt, D (-) para hydroxy phenyl glycine methyl ester, DL 2-(2-Chloro Phenyl) glycine, valine hydantoin, sodium phenoxy acetate, phenoxy acetic acid, phenyl acetic acid, potassium phenyl acetate, sodium phenyl acetate, benzaldehyde, benzyl chloride, benzyl alcohol, benzyl benzoate, benzyl cyanide, ortho chloro benzaldehyde, ortho chloro benzoic acid, ortho chloro benzyl cyanide, ortho chloro phenyl acetic acid, para chloro benzaldehyde, para chloro benzoic acid, para chloro benzyl cyanide, and D(-) phenyl glycine methyl ester hydrochloride; Agrochemical products include benzaldehyde, benzyl Chloride, ortho chloro benzaldehyde, ortho chloro benzoic acid, ortho chloro benzyl cyanide, para chloro benzaldehyde, para chloro benzoic acid, para chloro benzyl cyanide, and para chloro phenyl glycine; Fragrance/Perfumery products include sodium phenoxy acetate, phenoxy acetic acid, phenyl acetic acid, potassium phenyl acetate, sodium phenyl acetate, benzaldehyde, benzyl alcohol, benzyl benzoate, and di-benzyl ether; Dyes/Paints/Coatings products include benzaldehyde, benzyl alcohol, ortho chloro benzaldehyde, and para chloro benzaldehyde; and Other products include sodium benzoate, di-benzyl ether, sodium sulphate, ammonium sulphate, phosphoric acid, sodium nitrate, and hydrochloric acid. The company was incorporated in 2021 and is based in New Delhi, India. DCM Shriram Fine Chemicals Limited operates as a subsidiary of DCM Shriram Industries Limited.
