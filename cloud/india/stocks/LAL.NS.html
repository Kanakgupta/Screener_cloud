Company Overview

Lorenzini Apparels Limited designs, manufactures, and markets ready-made garments in India. It offers formal, semi-formal, and casual wear for men and women under the Monteil brand name. The company serves customers through retail outlets and e-commerce channels. The company was incorporated in 2007 and is based in New Delhi, India.
