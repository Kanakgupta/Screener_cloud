Company Overview

Safari Industries (India) Limited engages in the design, manufacturing, trading, and marketing of luggage, backpacks and travel accessories in India. The company offers hard, soft, laptop, and printed trolley bags; laptop, school, office, college, overnighters, adventure, formal, anti-theft, tech, and expandable storage backpacks; and accessories, such as messenger bags, duffle bags, sling bags, and neck pillows, as well as satchel bags. It sells its products under the Safari Select, Urban Jungle, and Carlton brands through hypermarkets, e-commerce and q-commerce platforms, multi-brand outlets, exclusive stores, Canteen Stores Department (CSD), direct-to-consumer channels, and institutional sales and export markets. Safari Industries (India) Limited was incorporated in 1980 and is based in Mumbai, India.
