Company Overview

Jubilant Agri and Consumer Products Limited manufactures and sells performance polymers, chemicals, and agri products in India and internationally. It operates through Performance Polymers & Chemicals, P&K Fertilizers, and Agri Nutrients segments. The company offers consumer products, including woodworking adhesives and wood finishes under the Jivanjor, Vamicol, Polystic, Charmwood, Hero, and Ultra Italia brands; and maintenance products comprising epoxy adhesive systems, sealants, instant adhesives, maintenance sprays, epoxy putty compounds, and paper glue products. It also provides food polymers, such as polyvinyl acetate for the chewing gum industry under the Vamipol 5, Vamipol 14, Vamipol 15, Vamipol 17, Vamipol 30, Vamipol 60, and Vamipol 100 brands; and Ester Gum that is used to produce the gum base for chewing and bubble gum under the Jubigum brand. In addition, the company offers vinyl pyridine latex, which is used in the dipping of automotive tire cords and conveyor belt fabrics under the Encord brand; and styrene butadiene and nitrile butadiene rubber latex for automotive tire manufacturers and tire cord dippers. Further, it provides crop nutrition products, such as single super phosphate fertilizer in both powder and granulated forms under the Ramban brand; and bio-stimulants. Additionally, the company produces and markets sulphuric acid as a chemical and plant growth regulator under the VAM-C brand; and provides plant macro and micronutrient products, including phosphorus, sulphur, zinc, calcium, boron, iron, copper, molybdenum, manganese, mycorrhiza, magnesium, and plant growth regulators. It also exports its products. The company was incorporated in 2008 and is based in Gurugram, India. Jubilant Agri and Consumer Products Limited operates as a subsidiary of Jubilant Industries Limited.
