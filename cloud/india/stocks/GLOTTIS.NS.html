Company Overview

Glottis Limited operates as a freight forwarding company. The company offers airport delivery and door-to-door delivery, express and priority cargo, freight consolidation, scheduled and charter flights, import and export customs clearance, end-to-end tracking, temperature-controlled air freight, dangerous goods handling, warehousing and distribution support, ecommerce air freight solutions, air freight insurance coverage, reverse logistics support, and multimodal connectivity; and LCL and FCL shipping, break bulk cargo handling, customs clearing and write-ups, port-to-door delivery and door-to-door delivery, marine cargo insurance, cross-trade shipping, vessel chartering, temperature-controlled shipping, real-time tracking, and warehousing and CFS services. It also provides breakbulk and breakbulk cargo handling, vessel chartering, personalized breakbulk chartering solutions, and weekly cargo consolidation, as well as oversized, heavy lift, and drayage services; inventory control, added services, cross-docking and transloading, order fulfillment for e-commerce, customs-approved warehousing, warehousing choices, reverse logistics, and supply chain solutions; and full truckload, less than load, and weekly cargo consolidation services. In addition, the company offers origin-to-destination delivery solutions, time-critical and priority cargo, freight consolidation, temperature-controlled logistics, warehousing and distribution solutions, and cargo insurance coverage; paperwork support, adaptive cargo handling solutions, deep cargo protection and insurance; accurate goods classification, clear duty and tax guidance, expedited customs processing, staying current with regulations, and specialized cargo support services; vessel chartering services, such as custom trips and time options, handling for special cargo, ship operations and management, paperwork and rules, and cargo insurance and investment. Glottis Limited was founded in 2004 and is based in Chennai, India.
