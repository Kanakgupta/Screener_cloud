Company Overview

ABM Knowledgeware Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides e-governance information security, technology-enabled applications through cloud services, and other software services in India. The company offers ABM MaiNet, an integrated ERP application to manage the working of a municipal corporation/council. It also provides SAP services, including citizen services, finance, e procurement, project monitoring and management, stores and materials management, vehicles and fleet management, management of real estate, HRMS and payroll management, and mobility solutions technology; CRM/ consumer facilitation centre (CFC); accrual based double entry accounting; e-Governance framework; tax reform solutions, such as assessment and billing, collection, and administration; and smart water management, including water billing, automated metering infrastructure, IOT platform for leakage and detection, and GIS and analytics. ABM Knowledgeware Limited was incorporated in 1993 and is based in Mumbai, India.
