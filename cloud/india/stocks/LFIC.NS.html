Company Overview

Lakshmi Finance & Industrial Corporation Limited, a non-banking finance company, invests in equity shares, mutual funds, and other securities in India. The company was formerly known as The Andhra Bank Ltd. and changed its name to Lakshmi Finance & Industrial Corporation Limited in April 1980. Lakshmi Finance & Industrial Corporation Limited was incorporated in 1923 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
