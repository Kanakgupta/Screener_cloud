Company Overview

EAAA India Alternatives Limited is an investment firm focused on Private Credit, Infrastructure, Real Estate, and Private Equity, specializing in Private Credit, Special Situation, Distressed Debt, Infrastructure Debt, Real Estate Debt, and Private Equity strategies. The firm primarily targets investments in office properties, mid-income and affordable housing developments, energy assets, renewable energy projects, climate mitigation and adaptation initiatives, operating infrastructure assets, and mid-stage operating companies. It focuses its investments in India. The firm seeks to provide structured credit and financing solutions to corporates, projects, or sponsors across the risk spectrum. It operates a diversified, multi-strategy platform focusing on yielding investments and debt financing. The firm provides credit solutions, special situations funding, real estate credit, investment-grade credit, infrastructure yield investments, commercial real estate yield acquisitions, perpetual capital, and energy transition funding. It looks to acquire, operate, and manage infrastructure assets with low counterparty risk, long residual tenures, and low operating costs. The firm also offers co-investment opportunities to its clients through portfolio management services. It manages alternative investment funds and offers both onshore and offshore investment solutions. EAAA India Alternatives Limited, formerly known as Edelweiss Alternative Asset Advisors Limited, is based in Mumbai, India, with additional offices in New Delhi, India; Gandhinagar, India; and Singapore.EAAA India Alternatives Limited operates as the operating subsidiary of Magnolia Commodities Services Private Limited.
