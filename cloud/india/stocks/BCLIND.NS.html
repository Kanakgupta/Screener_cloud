Company Overview

BCL Industries Limited, an agro-processing and manufacturing company, engages in the edible oil, rice milling, grain-based distillery, and real estate businesses in India. The company operates through three segments: Oil & Vanaspati, Distillery, and Real Estate. It provides oil and vanaspati ghee, liquor, extra neutral alcohol and ethanol, and other products. The company also constructs residential houses. The company was incorporated in 1976 and is based in Chandigarh, India.
