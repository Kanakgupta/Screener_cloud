Company Overview

SecureKloud Technologies Limited provides information and technology services in India and the United States. Its solutions include CloudEdge, a cloud foundation platform; Neutral Zone, a data collaboration platform; DataEdge, a cloud-based data analytics and AI engineering automation platform; readabl.ai, an AI powered solution that converts faxes, scanned documents, and blocks; and DocuGenieAI, an AI-native intelligent document automation platform. The company also provides advisory and consulting; cloud security and compliance; and cloud managed services comprising cloud deployment, cloud migration, cloud cost optimization, automation and DevOps, back-up and disaster recovery, infrastructure setup and management, and cloud monitoring and reporting, as well as strategic advisory, implementation, and development services. In addition, it offers contract, permanent, project-based IT staffing services. Further, the company offers Generative AI and LLM, robotic process automation, machine learning services, application modernization, cybersecurity, and blockchain solutions. It serves healthcare, life sciences, manufacturing, banking and financial, transport logistics sectors. The company was formerly known as 8K Miles Software Services Limited and changed its name to SecureKloud Technologies Limited in January 2021. SecureKloud Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is based in Chennai, India.
