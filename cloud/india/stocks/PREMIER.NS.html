Company Overview

Premier Limited manufactures and sells CNC machines and mechanical components for the wind energy and infrastructure sectors in India. The company engages in the engineering business, as well as provides professional and engineering-related services. Premier Limited was formerly known as The Premier Automobiles Ltd. and changed its name to Premier Limited in March 2005. The company was incorporated in 1944 and is based in Mumbai, India.
