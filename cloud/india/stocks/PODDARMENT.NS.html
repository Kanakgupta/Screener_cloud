Company Overview

Poddar Pigments Limited manufactures and sells color and additive masterbatches for dope dyeing of man-made fibers and various plastic applications in India and internationally. The company offers manmade fiber products, such as polyester, polypropylene, and nylon products; and masterbatches for plastic applications, including monolayer and multilayer films, biaxially oriented polypropylene/cast polypropylene film, non woven fabric, artificial grass, raffia/tape, shade net, PC Sheets, PP-R plumbing pipes, PLB ducts and corrugated pipes, jelly-filled telecommunication and halogen-free flame retardant cables, optical fiber cables, caps and closures, home essentials and appliances, furniture, luggage, pens and caps, toys, containers, drums, PET preforms, and bottles. It also provides functional and additive masterbatches comprising antiblock, antistatics, slip, antioxidant, optical brighteners, UV absorbers and stabilizers, clarifiers, diffusers, antifog, PPA, antimicrobial, flame retardant, hydrophilic and hydrophobic agent, anti scratch, easy dyeable polyester, and anti block. In addition, the company offers research and development, technical, and marketing support services; and experience center and digital shade card. It also exports its products. The company was incorporated in 1991 and is based in Chennai, India.
