Company Overview

Kalpataru Limited, through its subsidiaries, operates as a real estate development company in India. It engages in the real estate development, leasing, renting business; development lifecycle; and residential, comprising premium high-rise developments, integrated townships, and redevelopment projects in the Mumbai metropolitan region and Pune. The company was formerly known as Kalpataru Homes Limited and changed its name to Kalpataru Limited in February 2008. Kalpataru Limited was founded in 1969 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
