Company Overview

Aqylon Nexus Limited, a diversified technology company, provides AI solutions, space technology, semiconductor design, and datacenter infrastructure services in India and internationally. It serves aerospace, agriculture, telecommunications, and industrial automation sectors. The company was formerly known as Sri Adhikari Brothers Television Network Limited and changed its name to Aqylon Nexus Limited in January 2026. Aqylon Nexus Limited was founded in 1985 and is based in Mumbai, India.
