Company Overview

Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited provides stock brokerage services in India and internationally. The company offers equities, derivatives, alternative investment funds, IPOs, mutual funds, portfolio management services, investment basket, margin trading facility, NRI investing, commodities, currencies, fixed income, and insurance products. It also provides TradeMobi, an online trading app; TradeXpress; AR Invest, and mutual fund app; and Algozy for algo trading. The company was formerly known as Navratan Capital and Securities Limited and changed its name to Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited in January 2008. Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is based in Mumbai, India. Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited operates as a subsidiary of Anand Rathi Financial Services Limited.
