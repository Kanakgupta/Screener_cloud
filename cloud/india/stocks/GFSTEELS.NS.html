Company Overview

Grand Foundry Limited manufactures, exports, and sells bright steel bars in India, the United States, Japan, Singapore, Italy, Australia, and Canada. It also offers pickling and heat treatment lines. Grand Foundry Limited was incorporated in 1973 and is based in Mumbai, India.
