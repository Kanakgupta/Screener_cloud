Company Overview

Gabriel India Limited manufactures and sells of ride control products to the automotive industry in India, the Netherlands, and internationally. The company offers canister shock absorbers, telescopic front forks, inverted front forks, mono shox, floating piston shock absorbers, semi-active front forks, and semi-active shock absorbers; rear shock absorbers, strut assemblies, FSD suspension, semi-active suspension systems, and gas springs; axle dampers, cabin dampers, and seat dampers. It also provides double-acting hydraulic shock absorbers for conventional coaches, shock absorbers for EMU/MEMU coaches, dampers for diesel and electric locomotives, dampers for rajdhani and shatabdi coaches, and dampers for vande bharat coaches. In addition, the company offers macpherson struts, gas springs, brake pads, drive shafts, suspension parts, suspension and strut bush kits, OC springs, coolants, brake fluids, front fork components, oil seals, front fork oil, wheel rims, alloy wheels, spokes, cone sets, and trailing arms. Further, it provides e-bikes, solar dampers, and hydraulic and electronic products. The company also exports its products. The company was incorporated in 1961 and is based in Pune, India. Gabriel India Limited operates as a subsidiary of Asia Investments Private Limited.
