Company Overview

BN Agrochem Limited operates as a fast-moving consumer goods company in India. It engages in manufacturing and trading of various kinds of edible oil, oil seeds, solvent extraction, extracted oil-cakes, and refined oil. The company was formerly known as BN Holdings Limited and changed its name to BN Agrochem Limited in August 2025. BN Agrochem Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is based in Gautam Buddha Nagar, India.
