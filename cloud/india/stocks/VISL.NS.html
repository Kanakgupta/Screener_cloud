Company Overview

Vedanta Iron and Steel Limited engages in the exploration, mining, and processing of iron ore in India and West Africa. It produces steel, wire rods, TMT bars, billets, pig iron, ductile iron pipes, ferro-silicon, cement, and metallurgical coke. The company was founded in 1954 and is based in Panjim, India. Vedanta Iron and Steel Limited is a subsidiary of Vedanta Resources Limited.
