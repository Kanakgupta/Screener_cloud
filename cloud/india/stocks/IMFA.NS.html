Company Overview

Indian Metals and Ferro Alloys Limited engages in the production and sale of ferro chrome in India and internationally. It operates through Ferro Alloys, Power, and Mining segments. The company operates a captive power generation plant with a total capacity of 204.55MW; and two chrome ore mines, as well as a manufacturing plant for low-density aggregates and fly ash bricks for use in road construction and cement industries. It offers its products to stainless steel manufacturers and international traders and distributors. Indian Metals and Ferro Alloys Limited was incorporated in 1961 and is headquartered in Bhubaneswar, India.
