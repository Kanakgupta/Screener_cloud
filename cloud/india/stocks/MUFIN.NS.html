Company Overview

Mufin Green Finance Limited operates as a non-banking financial company in India. The company offers various loans for electric vehicles, electric charging infrastructure, and swappable batteries. It also provides operating or financial lease to fleet operators for the vehicles owned by the company. In addition, the company offers securities investment services; and solar financing and insurance premium financing services. It serves businesses, and small and medium enterprises. Mufin Green Finance Limited was formerly known as APM Finvest Limited. The company was incorporated in 2016 and is based in Delhi, India.
