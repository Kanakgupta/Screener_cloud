Company Overview

Raymond Realty Limited, through its subsidiaries, engages in the development and sale of housing projects in India. It offers residential and commercial properties, including apartments, contemporary high-rises, and retail complexes. Raymond Realty Limited was formerly known as Raymond Lifestyle Limited and changed its name to Raymond Realty Limited in January 2024. The company was founded in 1925 and is based in Thane, India. Raymond Realty Limited operates as a subsidiary of Raymond Limited.
