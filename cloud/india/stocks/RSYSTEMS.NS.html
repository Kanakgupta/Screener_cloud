Company Overview

R Systems International Limited, a digital product engineering company, designs and builds chip-to-cloud software products and platforms in Europe, the United States, and internationally. It provides information technology and business process outsourcing services. It also offers modernization solutions, such as legacy system and cloud modernization, hyper automation and managed services, and data and AI; and Cloud and DevOps solutions, including cloud migration and modernization, DevSecOps, CloudOps, FinOps, IoT, and AWS well-architected services; and chaos engineering integrated disaster recovery (DRaaS) models. In addition, it provides digital product engineering solutions that include cloud and DevOps, data and AI, automation and digital Ops, quality engineering, and mobile experience design; data and AI services comprising data strategy and architecture, data stack modernization, generative AI, business intelligence and data visualization, data product development, AI and ML, MLOps, and agentic AI; and automation and No-Code solutions. Further, the company offers embedded firmware services, including low level embedded system development and bottom layer, system software, middleware, and application engineering services, and pre and post silicon verification and validation; quality engineering services comprising comprehensive system, API and backend, and user acceptance testing, specialized testing protocols, test automation and optimization, performance testing excellence, and TOP testing suite; and mobile experience design services. Additionally, it provides enterprise packaged software and iPaaS services, such as advisory consulting, implementation and customization, cloud migration, upgrades, data migration and integration, analytics and reporting, and maintenance, support, and managed services. The company was incorporated in 1993 and is based in Greater Noida, India. R Systems International Limited is a subsidiary of BCP Asia II Topco II Pte. Ltd.
