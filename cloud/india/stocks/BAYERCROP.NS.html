Bayer CropScience Limited BAYERCROP NIFTY_SMALLNSEINFRA

Basic Materials · Agricultural Inputs · India
https://www.bayer.in

Bayer CropScience Limited engages in the manufacture, sale, and distribution of insecticides, fungicides, herbicides, and various other agrochemical products and hybrid corn seeds in India, Germany, Bangladesh, and internationally. It provides medicines in the therapeutic areas of cardiology, gynecology, diabetes, oncology, and ophthalmology; and computed tomography, X-Ray, and magnetic resonance imaging devices. In addition, the company offers consumer health products that are used as a daily health solution to treat minor illnesses, as well as sells and distributes row crop hybrid seeds. Further, it provides digital farming products comprising FarmRise and other digital applications, as well as tools, including remote sensing, IoT sensors, GPS, drones, artificial intelligence, and data analytics to monitor and manage crop growth, soil health, and weather conditions. The company also exports its products. Bayer CropScience Limited was founded in 1896 and is based in Thane, India.
