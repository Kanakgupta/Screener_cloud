Highlights
  • Broadcasting Reach: Operates a vast network of satellite television channels in multiple regional languages including Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, and Bangla.
  • Content Diversification: Offers a wide range of content genres including general entertainment, movies, music, news, and kids programming.
  • Sports Franchises: Owns and operates two cricket franchises: SunRisers Hyderabad in the Indian Premier League (IPL) and Sun Risers Eastern Cape in the South Africa Premier League.
  • Digital Presence: Operates SUNNXT, an Over-The-Top (OTT) platform, to cater to the growing digital viewership.
  • Print Media: Engages in the publication of daily newspapers and magazines, complementing its electronic media presence.
Strategic Focus

Sun TV Network is focused on strengthening its leadership in regional language broadcasting by investing in compelling content and expanding its digital footprint through the SUNNXT OTT platform. The company also aims to leverage its sports franchises for brand visibility and revenue diversification, while exploring new opportunities in media and entertainment.
