Company Overview

BLS E-Services Limited, a technology enabled digital service company, provides assisted E-services and E-governance services in India. The company act as business correspondents to provide banking products and services on behalf of banks to people, such as opening savings, recurring deposit accounts, cash deposits, withdrawals, remittance, transfer, and bill collection services, etc; and value added services through BLS touchpoints, including PoS services, ticketing services, and assisted e-commerce services. It also operates a network of access points that offers delivery of essential public utility, social welfare scheme, healthcare, financial, educational, agricultural, and banking services to governments and businesses to citizens in urban, semi-urban, rural, and remote areas. In addition, the company offers E-Services through retailers and digital stores; and facilitate delivery of various e-governance initiatives of the State Governments in India by providing various information communication technology enabled citizen centric services. The company was formerly known as BLS E-Services Private Limited and changed its name to BLS E-Services Limited in April 2023. BLS E-Services Limited was incorporated in 2016 and is based in Gurugram, India. BLS E-Services Limited operates as a subsidiary of BLS International Services Limited.
