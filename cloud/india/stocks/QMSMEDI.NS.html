Company Overview

QMS Medical Allied Services Limited distributes medical and healthcare devices in India. The company also manufactures and sells glucometers, pulse oximeters, IR thermometers, BP monitors, anatomy sets, neurology kits, orthopedic aids, and weighing scales, etc.; and operates QMSMEDS, an e-commerce platform that sells healthcare devices of various brands. In addition, the company provides diagnostic and patient management services, including ambulatory blood pressure tracking, continuous blood glucose and blood pressure/glucose monitoring, tonometry, and HbA1C services. Further, it offers Educamedics, which provides customized scientific content in the form of conferences, online courses, webinars, recorded lectures, and others. Additionally, the company offers chemical and chemical products, pharmaceuticals, and medicinal chemical and botanical products. QMS Medical Allied Services Limited was founded in 1994 and is based in Mumbai, India.
