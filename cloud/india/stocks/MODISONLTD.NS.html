Company Overview

Modison Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells electrical contacts for low, medium, and high voltage switchgear industries in India and internationally. It provides low voltage electrical contacts, such as silver tin oxide; silver brazing alloy; silver, steel, ni, and cupro nickel backed contracts; fine silver powder and granules; solid and bimetal rivets; silver cadmium oxide; silver graphite; silver inlay, edge lay, overlay, through lay, and top lay in copper and brass; silver tungsten and silver tungsten carbide; silver alloy wires; silver bimetal crown; and silver welded assembly for low voltage electrical contacts. The company also offers medium and high voltage electrical contacts, including eutectic brazing alloys; copper chromium discs; cast on plugs and segments; copper chromium zirconium contacts; copper tungsten and brazed arcing contracts; and electron-beam welding. In addition, it provides precious metal compounds comprising silver oxide, nitrate, and sulphate; and silver bullion products, such as casted silver, minted silver, and silver cutlery. Further, the company offers refining and recovery of precious metals; melting and assaying of silver; silver plating; electron beam welding; and research and development of contract materials for special applications. It serves the industrial motors, transmission and distribution, railway, aerospace, automotive, marine, defense, engineering, life science, and pharmaceutical industries. The company was formerly known as Modison Metals Limited and changed its name to Modison Limited in July 2022. Modison Limited was founded in 1965 and is based in Vapi, India.
