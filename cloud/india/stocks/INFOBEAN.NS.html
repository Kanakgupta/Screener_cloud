Company Overview

InfoBeans Technologies Limited designs, builds, and manages digital applications in the United States, Germany, India, the United Arab Emirates, and internationally. The company offers Expona AI 2.0, a context trained AI agent for conversational intelligence; BeanTrail, an AI-driven Salesforce audit intelligence for critical field history insights; Spacewarp, an AI rules-based tool for ServiceNow; and GCC services for end-to-end setup, transformation, and managed services. It also provides agentic AI, an enterprise-grade development and deployment of scalable AI agents; managed support for AI-enabled L2/L3 support driving year-over-year cost reduction; InsaneSDD 2.0, a software creation with AI native spec driven development; RAI for AI-led engineering and trusted deployments; and Stanza, a committee management, voting, and balloting platform. InfoBeans Technologies Limited was founded in 2000 and is headquartered in Indore, India.
