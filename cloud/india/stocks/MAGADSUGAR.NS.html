Company Overview

Magadh Sugar & Energy Limited manufactures and sells sugar and its by-products in India and internationally. It operates in three segments: Sugar, Distillery, and Co-Generation. The company offers white crystal sugar, ethanol, industrial spirits, and bio-compost; and by-products, including molasses and press-mud. It also generates and transmits bagasse-based power. The company exports its products. Magadh Sugar & Energy Limited was founded in 1932 and is headquartered in Kolkata, India.
