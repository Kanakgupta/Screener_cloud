Company Overview

G.M. Breweries Limited manufactures and sells alcoholic beverages in India. The company offers Indian made foreign liquor and country liquor. It offers its products under the G.M.SANTRA, G.M.DOCTOR, G.M.LIMBU PUNCH, and G.M.DILBAHAR SOUNF brand names. The company was incorporated in 1981 and is based in Mumbai, India.
