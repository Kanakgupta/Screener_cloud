Company Overview

Garuda Construction and Engineering Limited offers civil construction services in India. It constructs residential, residential cum commercial, hospitality, infrastructure, and commercial and industrial projects. The company also constructs concrete building structures and composite steel structures. In addition, it provides operations and maintenance services; and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing services, as well as finishing works. The company was incorporated in 2010 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India. Garuda Construction and Engineering Limited is a subsidiary of PKH Ventures Limited.
