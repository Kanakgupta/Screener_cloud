Company Overview

Supreme Engineering Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of steel products in India. The company's Special Steels division manufactures alloys, duplex stainless steels, tool steels, high speed steels, and stainless steels. Its Wire division manufactures carbon, tool, stainless, and alloy steels. The company is also involved in the heavy engineering activities, as well as exports its products. It serves the oil and gas, automotive, hand-tools, industrial machinery, aerospace, heavy engineering, defense, space, and power sectors. The company was formerly known as Supreme Engineering Private Limited. Supreme Engineering Limited was incorporated in 1987 and is based in Navi Mumbai, India.
