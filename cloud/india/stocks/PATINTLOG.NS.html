Company Overview

Patel Integrated Logistics Limited provides logistics services in India. The company engages in the co-loading of air freight; and provision of surface transport, warehousing, air cargo consolidation, and other value-added services. It is also involved in providing gym subscriptions, and sale of health supplements. The company was founded in 1959 and is based in Mumbai, India.
