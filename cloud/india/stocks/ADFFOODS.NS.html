Company Overview

ADF Foods Limited produces and exports ethnic and convenience food products in India and internationally. It operates through Process and Preserved Food; and Distribution Business segments. The company offers ready-to-eat meals; food accompaniments, such as pickles, chutneys, dips, cooking and condiment pastes, dipping and cooking sauces, spices, and other processed food products; frozen food, including snacks, parathas, samosas, Indian breads, Kathi rolls, wraps, curries, fruits, vegetables, sweets, and cooking paste cubes; condiments; marinades; curry powder; tamarind-based powder and slabs; pulp; rice; murabba; canned products; flavored milk drinks; pulses, staples, and flours; and organic and plant-based products. It offers its products under the Khansaama, Ashoka, Truly Indian, ADF Soul, Camel, and Aeroplane brands. The company sells its products to retail consumers, wholesalers, and distributors through supermarkets, ethnic food stores, quick commerce platforms, and online channels. It exports its products. The company was formerly known as American Dryfruits Ltd. and changed its name to ADF Foods Limited in March 2000. ADF Foods Limited was founded in 1932 and is based in Mumbai, India.
