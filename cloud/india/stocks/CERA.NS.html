Company Overview

Cera Sanitaryware Limited manufactures, sells, and trades in building material products in India. The company offers sanitaryware, including toilets, wash basins, cisterns, seat covers, and fittings; faucets, showers, health faucets, allied products, and bath accessories; urinals; special needs bathroom products, shower chairs, and grab bars and handrails; green bathroom products; floor and wall tiles; shower partitions; kitchen sinks; bathtubs; and vanity cabinets and mirrors under the Senator, CERA Luxe, CERA, and Polipluz brands. It also exports its products. The company serves individual homeowners, small-scale buyers, construction companies, real estate developers, government bodies, and large entities through dealer and sub-dealer networks and direct engagement channels. The company was formerly known as Madhusudan Oils & Fats Ltd and changed its name to Cera Sanitaryware Limited in January 2003. Cera Sanitaryware Limited was founded in 1980 and is headquartered in Ahmedabad, India.
