Company Overview

Hexa Tradex Limited operates as a dealer, trader, import and export agent, representative, contractor, buyer, seller, and broker in India. The company operates through two segments: Trading and Other Activities; and Investment and Finance. The company is involved in the trading of chemicals, iron and steel products, machinery parts and accessories, minerals, motor vehicle components, non-ferrous metals and related products, obsolete and discarded materials, paints, varnishes and adhesives, and other items. It also engages in buying and selling, as well as in trading of products. Hexa Tradex Limited was incorporated in 2010 and is based in New Delhi, India.
