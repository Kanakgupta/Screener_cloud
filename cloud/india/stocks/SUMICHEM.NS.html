Company Overview

Sumitomo Chemical India Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of household and public health insecticides, agricultural pesticides, and animal nutrition products in India and internationally. It also offers crop protection products, such as insecticides, herbicides, fungicides, and miticides; household pesticides; animal nutrition; plant nutrition and plant growth regulators; fumigants and rodenticides; and biological products. The company was incorporated in 2000 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India. Sumitomo Chemical India Limited is a subsidiary of Sumitomo Chemical Company Limited.
