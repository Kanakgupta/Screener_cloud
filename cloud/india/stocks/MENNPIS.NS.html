Company Overview

Menon Pistons Limited manufactures and sells auto components in India. It offers powertrain parts, including piston rings, plain cast aluminium pistons, cast iron pistons articulated pistons, FCD/ductile iron pistons, single and double alfin pistons, oil-cooled gallery pistons, single piece forged steel pistons, friction welded steel pistons and gudgeon pins; and engine valvetrain parts, such as rocker arms, rocker levers, rocker arm shafts, precision rollers, precision pins, and lifter bodies. The company also provides hydraulic power systems comprising control valve spools, plungers, valve housings, slider rings, and oil control valve assemblies; high-precision turned parts, including bar-route shaft components, injector assembly parts, and power steering assemblies; and precision machined castings products. It serves agricultural, compressor, defense, commercial, and off highway clients, as well as diesel engines, cars, and LCVs/HCVs. Menon Pistons Limited was founded in 1969 and is based in Kolhapur, India.
