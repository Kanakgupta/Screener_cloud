Company Overview

Mirza International Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures, markets, and sells leather footwear products in India, the United Kingdom, the United States, and internationally. It operates in two segments, Footwear and Tannery. The company offers leather shoes and boots under the Thomas Crick brand name; women's shoes under the Off the Hook brand name; and men's leather footwear under the Oaktrak brand name. It exports its products. The company was formerly known as Mirza Tanners Ltd. and changed its name to Mirza International Limited in August 2005. Mirza International Limited was incorporated in 1979 and is headquartered in Noida, India.
