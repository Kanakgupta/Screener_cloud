Company Overview

NIIT Learning Systems Limited provides managed training services in India, the United States, Europe, and internationally. Its managed training services include custom content and curriculum design, learning administration and delivery, strategic sourcing, learning technology, and learning and development (L&D) consulting services. The company also offers specialized learning solutions, such as AI-powered L&D transformation, digital and IT training, immersive learning, customer education, talent pipeline as a service, and HCM technology and consulting services, as well as leadership and professional development training services; and generative artificial intelligence learning services. It serves technology and telecom; consulting and professional service; life sciences and healthcare; energy and commodities; aerospace and aviation; banking and financial service; manufacturing and industrial; consumer products and retail; insurance; humanitarian and NGO; real estate; and automotive industries. The company was formerly known as Mindchampion Learning Systems Limited and changed its name to NIIT Learning Systems Limited in January 2022. NIIT Learning Systems Limited was founded in 1981 and is based in Gurugram, India.
