Company Overview

Celebrity Fashions Limited engages in the designing, manufacturing, sale, and export of garments and clothing accessories in India and internationally. The company offers shirts, t-shirts, sweaters, shackets, trousers, shorts, and denims for men and boys under the Indian Terrain brand through retail outlets and e-commerce stores. Celebrity Fashions Limited was incorporated in 1988 and is based in Chennai, India.
