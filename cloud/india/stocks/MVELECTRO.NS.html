Company Overview

MV Electrosystems Limited engages in research, design, development, and manufacturing of electrical and power electronics equipment used in railway rolling stock, railway propulsion equipment, and cable assemblies. The company provides rolling stock electrics and propulsion systems, including IGBT based traction converter, auxiliary power converters, vehicle control units/ TCMS, driver display unit (DDU); switchgear and panels; cable protection and interconnect products; overhead electrification products; traction motors and transformers; and embedded systems. The company was incorporated in 2009 and is based in Faridabad, India.
