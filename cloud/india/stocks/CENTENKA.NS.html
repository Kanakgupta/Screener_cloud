Company Overview

Century Enka Limited produces and sells synthetic yarns and related products in India and internationally. The company provides nylon filament yarns, such as monofilament, mother, multifilament, and tow yarns; and fully drawn, partially and high oriented, drawn textured, and air textured yarns, draw winder, and jumbo beam yarns. It also offers nylon tyre cord fabrics, including yarn, greige fabrics, and dipped fabrics for reinforcement of tires, which are used in motorcycles, scooters, light commercial vehicles, heavy commercial vehicles, and off the road vehicles. The company was incorporated in 1965 and is based in Pune, India.
