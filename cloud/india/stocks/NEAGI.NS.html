Company Overview

Neelamalai Agro Industries Limited cultivates, manufactures, sells, and exports tea in India and internationally. The company operates Katary and Sutton estates located in the Nilgiris District in Tamil Nadu. Neelamalai Agro Industries Limited was incorporated in 1943 and is based in Chennai, India.
