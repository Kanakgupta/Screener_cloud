Company Overview

Genus Power Infrastructures Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides smart metering solutions in India and internationally. The company offers various electricity meters, including residential, commercial, and industrial meters; prepayment and smart meters; distribution transformer metering products; non-utility business or segment meters, such as panel, and net ABT meters; and open access/grid meters. It also provides smart grid products, such as smart meters; smart city, including smart group metering products and smart streetlight management systems; advanced metering infrastructure service providers; communication devices comprising optical probes, cellular modems, communication modules, and smart gateways; meter data management systems; and head end systems, as well as renewable metering products; gas meters, which include smart and meter interface unit intellilog (g-Setu); ultrasonic water meter (SALIL); prepayment meters; and data loggers. In addition, the company undertakes turnkey power projects for switchyard/substations, distribution/transmission lines, and rural electrification; and offers software solutions, including work force management, base computer software, smart meter mobile application, meter data management system, and prepaid vending software. Further, it provides hybrid microcircuits for various applications, such as telecommunication, consumer, IT products, power electronics, automotive, industrial, R-networks, and other applications. The company exports its products to Australia, Bhutan, Hong Kong, Nepal, New Zealand, Nigeria, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates. Genus Power Infrastructures Limited was incorporated in 1992 and is based in Jaipur, India.
