Company Overview

Karma Energy Limited generates and sells power from renewable sources in India. The company develops and operates wind farms with a capacity of 33 MW in the states of Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu. It also develops a 3.5 MW hydro power project in Balij-ka-Nala-II, Chamba District. The company was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Mumbai, India.
