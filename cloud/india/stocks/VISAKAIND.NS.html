Company Overview

Visaka Industries Limited manufactures and sells manufacture of cement fibre sheets, fibre cement boards and panels, solar panels, and synthetic yarn in India and internationally. It operates through two segments, Building Products and Synthetic Yarn. The company provides fibre cement boards for interior and semi-exposed applications; integrated solar roofing systems; cement roofing sheets, such as close-fitting adjustable ridges, apron pieces, north light ridges and curves, barge boards, serrated adjustable ridges, roof lights, and ridge finials; and the Wonder yarn. It also offers green EV charging station; and trades in green, eco-friendly, sustainable, and organic products. The company was incorporated in 1981 and is based in Secunderabad, India.
