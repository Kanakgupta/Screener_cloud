Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited UTKARSHBNK NSEFINANCIAL

Financial Services · Banks - Regional · India
https://www.utkarsh.bank.in

Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited provides banking and financial services for individuals and businesses in India. It operates through Corporate/Wholesale Banking, Retail Banking, Treasury, and Other Banking Operations segments. The company offers savings, salary, current, term and recurrent deposit, institutional, and escrow banking accounts; term deposit products; certificates of deposits; locker facilities; digital products; investment products; joint liability group, business, MSME, commercial vehicle and construction equipment, gold, personal, home and other loans; overdraft against fixed deposit, wholesale lending, LAP retail, and Micro LAP; inward and outward remittance; and bill payment services. It also provides ATM, branch, internet, and mobile banking services; micro and wholesale banking services; mutual funds; and life, health, and general insurance. The company was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Varanasi, India. Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited is a subsidiary of Utkarsh CoreInvest Limited.
