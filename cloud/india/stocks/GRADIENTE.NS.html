Company Overview

Gradiente Infotainment Limited operates as a media and entertainment company, providing services in advertising, television program production, films, event management, business communication, public relations, direct marketing, and web media. The company offers advertising, film production, television program creation, and event management services, along with business and financial communication, public relations, direct marketing, and web media solutions. It produces serials and televised dramas for television channels, focusing on entertainment and social issues. The company provides a digital platform, Gradiente Digital, with features such as customization options, user interface, integration capabilities, real-time updates, notifications, analytics, and reporting, as well as support and maintenance services. The company serves clients from various industry verticals, including advertising, films, television, event management, business and financial communication, public relations, direct marketing, web media, and investors seeking passive investment opportunities. The company was founded in 1992 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
