Company Overview

HB Stockholdings Limited, a non-banking financial and non-deposit taking company, engages in the investment in securities business in India. The company involved in undertaking of financial services; sale and purchase of securities; and dealing in various kinds of securities. The company was formerly known as HB Portfolio Leasing Limited and changed its name to HB Stockholdings Limited in February 1997. HB Stockholdings Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is headquartered in Gurugram, India.
