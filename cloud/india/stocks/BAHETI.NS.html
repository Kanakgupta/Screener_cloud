Company Overview

Baheti Recycling Industries Limited, an aluminum recycling company, primarily engages in processing aluminum-based metal scrap in India. The company offers aluminium alloy ingots; and aluminium de-ox, such as aluminium notch bar, shots, cubes, and ingots. The company serves the automotive, steel manufacturing, electrical transmission, construction, packaging, consumer durables, and industrial manufacturing sectors. The company was formerly known as Baheti Metal And Ferro Alloys Limited and changed its name to Baheti Recycling Industries Limited in January 2022. Baheti Recycling Industries Limited was incorporated in 1994 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
