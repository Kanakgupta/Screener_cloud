Company Overview

ArMee Infotech Limited provides IT Infrastructure solutions and IT managed services for Government, PSUs, BFSI, education, and private sectors in India. It offers IT Infrastructure products, such as computers, servers, interactive panels and their peripherals; IT managed services, installation and integration of IT hardware and software products; Renewable Energy Space, such as renewable energy EPC services, renewable energy Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) services, renewable energy Battery Energy Storage System (BESS); and experience zones, engages in selling IT hardware and consumer goods, including computers and computer peripherals, consumer electricals, gaming products, and electronics. It also offers cyber-security solutions, including firewall, antivirus, intrusion detection/prevention systems, and encryption services; technical manpower; skill development training; annual maintenance services onsite and off-site operational support; and IT infrastructure maintenance services. The company was founded in 2003 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
