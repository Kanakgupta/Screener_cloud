Company Overview

Gayatri Projects Limited engages in the construction business in India. The company is involved in engineering, procurement, and construction of roads, irrigation, water distribution, and mining works, as well as industrial, transportation, and land development projects. It also engages in the ownership of infrastructure assets. The company was founded in 1963 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
