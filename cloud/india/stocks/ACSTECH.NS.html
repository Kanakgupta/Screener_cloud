Company Overview

ACS Technologies Limited provides system integration, security and surveillance, IOT solutions, and software products and services in India and internationally. The company provides smart transport, smart energy, smart water, smart surveillance, smart emergency response, information services, intelligent advertising, and home automation solutions; IoT and Automation products for homes, offices, apartments, gated communities, campuses, townships, and smart cities; LMS, a software application for administration, documentation, tracking, reporting of training programs, classrooms and online events, e-learning programs, and training content; and intranet based document archival, retrieval and management system. It also offers property management system, a tool for reservation process, guest registration, accounts, check-in/check-out, discounts, allowances, and the audits for hospitality management; queue management tool that automates the task of token issue and scheduling for visitors; and visitor management system, a solution for visitor management, including appointments, issue of pass, visitor details storage, time in/out, and MIS reporting features. Further, the company provides HelpDesk, an online intranet/cloud solution that facilitates online registration of complaint or issue, assignment to staff, status tracking, and closure; and human resource management system, an employee management system for hiring indent, recruitment process, onboarding, attendance, payroll, appraisals, performance monitoring, resignations, and exit management. The company was formerly known as LN Industries India Limited and changed its name to ACS Technologies Limited in June 2023. ACS Technologies Limited was founded in 1980 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
