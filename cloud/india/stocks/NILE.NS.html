Company Overview

Nile Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of pure lead for battery consumption in India and internationally. The company provides pure lead antimony, lead selenium, lead calcium, lead tin, and pure lead. It serves its products to manufacturers of lead acid batteries, PVC stabilizers, and lead-oxide. Nile Limited was incorporated in 1984 and is based in Hyderabad, India.
