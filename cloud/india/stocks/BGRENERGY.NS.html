Company Overview

BGR Energy Systems Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells capital equipment for power plants, petrochemical and process industries, and refineries in India and internationally. The company is involved in the provision of turnkey engineering, procurement, and construction services for power plants; and supply of sub and super critical steam turbines and generators, and single and multiple pressure level natural circulation heat recovery steam generators; and providing process coolers, finned tubes, radiator and compressors coolers, and tube bundles. It also offers turnkey solutions for EHV substations; optical fiber composite ground wire and optical line termination equipment; gas insulated switchgear substations; EHV transmission lines; plant electrical projects; and rural electrification, as well as power trading and balance of plant works for hydro projects. In addition, the company provides process equipment packages and systems, including phase separator system / skids, indirect water bath heater, gas conditioning, regulating and metering systems, knockout drums / scrubbers, and filter separators / dry gas filters; pipeline equipment; compressor packaging products; and storage tanks; deaerators, membrane based water treatment plant, membrane based desalination plants, effluent treatment and recycling plants, and condensate polishing units. In addition, it operates thermal power projects at Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, and Chhattishgarh. BGR Energy Systems Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is based in Chennai, India.
