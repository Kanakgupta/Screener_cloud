Company Overview

Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Limited manufactures and distributes various food products in India and internationally. The company provides biscuits, including cookies, creams, crackers, digestives, and glucose under the Mrs. Bector's Cremica brand. It also offers bakery products that includes breads, buns, kulchas, rusk, pizza bases, and cakes under the English Oven brand. The company exports its products. Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Limited was founded in 1978 and is based in Gurugram, India.
