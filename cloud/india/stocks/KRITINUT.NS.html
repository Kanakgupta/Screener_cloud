Company Overview

Kriti Nutrients Limited manufactures and sells soya-based products under the Kriti brand in India and internationally. The company offers edible oils, such as refined sunflower oil, refined soybean oil, filtered groundnut oil, and mustard oil; soya chunks; soya lecithin; protein-based nuggets/TVP; full fat soya flour; defatted flakes, grits, and flour (toasted and untoasted); super HyPro SBM; soya hulls; acid oil and fatty acids; and other value-added soybean derivatives and protein products. It serves the food, pharmaceutical, aqua and poultry, dairy, and infant food industries. The company exports its products. The company was founded in 1992 and is based in Indore, India. Kriti Nutrients Limited operates as a subsidiary of Sakam Trading Private Limited.
