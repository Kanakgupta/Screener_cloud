Company Overview

Usha Martin Education & Solutions Limited provides educational services primarily in India. The company provides ERP certification courses; big data analytics training and certification; Hybris training and certification programs; industry connect services; university connect services; and career development programs. It also operates training centers for various higher education courses under the Usha Martin Academy brand name. The company was formerly known as Usha Martin Infotech Limited and changed its name to Usha Martin Education & Solutions Limited in 2009. Usha Martin Education & Solutions Limited was incorporated in 1997 and is based in Kolkata, India.
