Company Overview

Orchasp Limited provides computer programming, consultancy, and related services in India. The company provides application development and automation services, cloud enablement, cyber security, artificial intelligence, blockchain in healthcare, internet of things, business process services, and staffing augmentation services. It also offers transaction processing, data management, integration with third party tools and applications, independent testing and validation, network and surveillance, and IT Support and analytics. It serves health care, telecom, manufacturing, engineering, energy, retail, and railways industries. The company was formerly known as Cybermate Infotek Limited. Orchasp Limited was incorporated in 1994 and is based in Secunderabad, India.
