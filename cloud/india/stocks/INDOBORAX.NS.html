Company Overview

Indo Borax & Chemicals Limited manufactures and sells boron-based products in India. It offers borax products, such as decahydrate crystals and granular; boric acid products that include boric acid Indian pharmacopoeia grade, and technical grade granular, and powders; boron oxide; and disodium octaborate tetrahydrate. The company was incorporated in 1980 and is based in Mumbai, India.
