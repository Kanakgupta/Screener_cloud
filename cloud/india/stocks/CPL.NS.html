Company Overview

Captain Polyplast Limited manufactures, sells, exports, and trades in micro irrigation systems and allied products in India. The company also engaged on the DCA cum CS basis of Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. for Polymer Business. The company provides irrigation equipment, such as drip irrigation systems, drippers and laterals, and sprinklers; irrigation accessories comprising compression fittings, control valves, irrigation filters, fertigation equipment, greenhouses, and mulch films; solar systems consisting of solar water pumps, mulch films, solar fence energizers, solar water heaters, and solar streetlight systems; water-soluble fertilizers; and pipes and fittings. It also offers high density polyethylene, linear low density polyethylene, polypropylene homopolymer, and polypropylene copolymer polymer products under the Propel brand for various applications across packaging, pipes, films, and molding, etc. In addition, the company provides disc, screen, hydro-cyclone, and sand filters; fertilizer tanks; digital controllers; and pressure gauges, etc. Further, it installs micro-irrigation systems; and offers agronomical services. Captain Polyplast Limited was incorporated in 1997 and is headquartered in Rajkot, India.
