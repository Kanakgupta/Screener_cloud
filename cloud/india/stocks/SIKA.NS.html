Company Overview

Sika Interplant Systems Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in manufacturing and rendering of services in engineering products and engineering projects/systems for aerospace, defense and space, and automotive sectors in India. The company offers search and rescue products, such as rescue hoists/winches and air ambulance/EMS modules; landing gear and hydraulics products; and interconnection systems, including harnesses and looms, electro mechanical assemblies, electronic module integration, IE squared facilities, and environmental stress screening. It also provides dipping sonar winch, rescue hoists/winches, cargo hooks, cargo winches, and marine winches; and airborne handling systems, air intake particle separator, and landing gear and hydraulics. In addition, the company offers various services, including design, assembly, integration, and supply; supply chain management; maintenance, repairs/refits, and life support; structuring and managing agreements with collaborators, partners, and suppliers; product upgrades; and spares and logistics support services. The company was founded in 1969 and is based in Bengaluru, India. Sika Interplant Systems Limited is a subsidiary of Ultraweld Engineers Private Limited.
