Company Overview

RACL Geartech Limited manufactures and sells automotive components. The company offers pin gear T49; actuator shaft; assy diff, bevel gear FRT; balancer, clutch, output, and input shaft assembly; bearing fixed pin; counter balancer gear and shaft; driven pulley shaft; gear timing drive LH, EGR rear; gear wheel with spiral; journal; primary gear; rider foot peg comp RH; tab Ay driving gear timing drive RH; torque limiter assembly; and wheel gear with I.P. used for 2 wheelers. It also provides gears and shafts for agriculture vehicles and machineries; high pressure pump drive gear, gear assembly-idle, counter shaft and main drive, and drive shaft for commercial vehicles; intermediate shaft, park lock, parking interlock, and ring gear, and planet carrier used for passenger vehicles; and assembly rear axle, eccentric and eccentric housing, gear primary driven, output shaft, pinion gear, rotor shaft, and wheel axle, flange, and shaft for e-mobility purposes. In addition, the company offers anti backlash gear 75T, balance shaft assembly, clutch gear 66T, compressor shaft 17T, drive gear 21T, driving gear 42T, matting gear 57T, primary drive gear 52T, primary gear 46T assy, hollow wheel, and drive shaft assembly used for all-terrain vehicles; electric switch gears, such as drum complete and main shaft; and cluster gear and secondary gear shaft for 3 wheelers. The company serves original equipment manufacturers, including manufacturers of cars, motorcycles, scooters and commercial vehicles, tier-1 suppliers, off-highway vehicles, agricultural machinery manufacturers, and construction equipment manufactures. It operates in India, Austria, Japan, Germany, Switzerland, Italy, Thailand, Vietnam, the United States, Mexico, North America, China, and Sweden. The company was formerly known as Raunaq Automotive Components Limited and changed its name to RACL Geartech Limited in October 2015. RACL Geartech Limited was incorporated in 1983 and is headquartered in Noida, India.
