Company Overview

Marsons Limited engages in manufacturing, supplying, erecting, testing, and commissioning of power and distribution transformers in India. The company offers distribution, power, unitized transportable substation, dry type, cast resin, solar, and instrumental transformers, as well as furnace transformers comprising arc, submerged, ladle, induction, and DC arc furnace transformers. It also provides after sales services. The company was incorporated in 1976 and is based in Kolkata, India.
