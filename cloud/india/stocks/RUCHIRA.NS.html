Company Overview

Ruchira Papers Limited manufactures and markets kraft paper, and writing and printing paper products in India and internationally. The company offers white writing and printing papers that are used for fabrication of spiral notebooks and writing materials; and colored papers used in the fabrication of spiral notebooks, wedding cards, shade cards, children's coloring books, and bill books, as well as disposable bowls and cups for hot and cold beverages. It also provides copier paper for commercial offices and copying service providers; Kraft papers for the production of corrugated board boxes, composite cans, paper bags, fibre drums, textile cones/ream wrappers, food packaging, and other packaging application. In addition, the company offers packaging solutions. The company offers its products under Mogra, Leher, Kora, Sitara, Safeda, Gehua, Tarang, Mela, Baadal, Rozaana, Maati, Multani, Khadi, and Badami brands. Ruchira Papers Limited was incorporated in 1980 and is based in Yamuna Nagar, India.
