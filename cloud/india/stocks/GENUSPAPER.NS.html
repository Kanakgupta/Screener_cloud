Genus Paper & Boards Limited GENUSPAPER NSEINFRA

Basic Materials · Paper & Paper Products · India
https://www.genuspaper.com

Genus Paper & Boards Limited primarily manufactures and sells kraft paper in India and internationally. It operates through Paper Business, Coke Business, and Strategic Investment Activity segments. The company offers carton boards, container boards, speciality kraft and boards, writing printing paper, and duplex boards, as well as other specialty papers. It also invests in shares and securities, and loans. The company serves FMCG, consumer durables, pharmaceuticals, garments, textile, footwear, e-commerce, and notebook and publication industries. Genus Paper & Boards Limited was incorporated in 1996 and is based in New Delhi, India.
