Company Overview

Vardhman Polytex Limited manufactures and sells yarns and garments in India and internationally. The company operates through Textiles and Real Estate segments. It offers carded, combed, organic, BCI, and SUPIMA cotton yarns; cotton polyester yarns; and grey and dyed value-added yarns. Vardhman Polytex Limited was incorporated in 1980 and is based in Ludhiana, India.
