Company Overview

Wonder Electricals Limited engages in the manufacture and supply of ceiling, exhaust, pedestal, and TPW and BLDC fans in India. It also exports its products. The company was formerly known as Wonder Fibromats Limited and changed its name to Wonder Electricals Limited in December 2022. Wonder Electricals Limited was founded in 2003 and is based in New Delhi, India.
