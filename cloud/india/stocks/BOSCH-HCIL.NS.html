Bosch Home Comfort India Limited BOSCH-HCIL NSE

Consumer Cyclical · Furnishings, Fixtures & Appliances · India
https://www.hitachiaircon.in

Bosch Home Comfort India Limited manufactures and distributes air conditioners, chillers, refrigerators, air purifiers, and variable refrigerant flow systems in India and internationally. It operates through Cooling Products for Comfort and Commercial Use; and Design and Development Services segments. The company offers inverter and non-inverter, fixed speed, split, non-inverter, hot and cold, packaged, window, and cassette air conditioners (AC); ducted and ductless air conditioners; centralized AC and room AC controllers; and water- and air-cooled screw chillers, and centrifugal chillers, as well as air quality solutions, and AC apps and controllers. It also provides after-sales services. The company offers its products under the Hitachi brand. It also exports its products. The company was formerly known as Hitachi Home & Life Solutions (India) Limited and changed its name to Johnson Controls-Hitachi Air Conditioning India Limited in September 2016. The company was formerly known as Johnson Controls-Hitachi Air Conditioning India Limited and changed its name to Bosch Home Comfort India Limited in November 2025. The company was incorporated in 1984 and is headquartered in Kadi, India. Johnson Controls-Hitachi Air Conditioning India Limited is a subsidiary of JCHAC India Holdco Limited.
