Company Overview

Mold-Tek Technologies Limited provides civil and mechanical design engineering services in India and internationally. It provides construction services, including structural steel detailing, connection design and delegated design, precast detailing, miscellaneous steel detailing, structural steel estimating, and pre engineered steel building design services; manufacturing services, such as automotive design, special purpose machine design, and sheet metal press tool designs services; utilities consisting telecom tower structure design, and miscellaneous design services; and plant engineering, including process, piping, mechanical, civil and structural, electrical, and instrumentation engineering services. The company serves construction, fabrication, automotive, and EPC companies. Mold-Tek Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is headquartered in Hyderabad, India.
