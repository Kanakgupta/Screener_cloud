Core Focus

Bajaj Finserv is the holding entity for Bajaj Finance + Bajaj Allianz (General & Life Insurance). AI is deployed across insurance underwriting (telematics-based motor insurance pricing), health insurance claim scrutiny (AI fraud detection), and Bajaj Finserv App (AI-personalised financial product marketplace for 70M+ users).

Key Initiatives
  • AI across the Bajaj financial ecosystem As the holding company for Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Allianz, Bajaj Finserv deploys AI in lending underwriting, insurance pricing and claims, and customer acquisition across a vast franchise.
  • Data-driven cross-sell engine Machine-learning models mine a large customer base to cross-sell loans, insurance and investments, raising lifetime value per customer.
  • AI in insurance underwriting & claims Bajaj Allianz uses AI for risk pricing, fraud detection and faster claims settlement, improving combined ratios.
