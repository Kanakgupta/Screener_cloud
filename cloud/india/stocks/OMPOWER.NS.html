Company Overview

Om Power Transmission Limited, an engineering, procurement, and construction company, executes high-voltage and extra-high voltage transmission line projects and substations. The company constructs transmission lines of voltage levels; undertakes underground cabling projects for high tension and low tension power systems providing services, such as trenching, cable laying, jointing, and termination; and offers end-to-end services for substation projects comprising design, supply, erection, testing, commissioning, and civil works for conventional and renewable energy evacuation substations. It also provides operation and maintenance services for substations and transmission lines. The company serves power utilities and grid operators, renewable energy developers and solar park operators, industrial and corporate users, transportation and infrastructure projects, and operation and maintenance clients. Om Power Transmission Limited was incorporated in 2011 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
