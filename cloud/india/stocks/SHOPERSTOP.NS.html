Company Overview

Shoppers Stop Limited engages in the retailing of household and consumer products through departmental stores under the Shoppers Stop brand in India. The company offers women's Indian wear for weddings, festivals, and occasions under the Kashish brand; festive and ceremonial ethnic wear for men under the Bandeya brand; Smart casual products under the Fratini brands; family fashion products under the Stop brand; and Western-inspired denim and casual wear under the Life brand. It also provides girls' wear products under the Fratini girl brand; clothing for young children under the karrot brand; innerwear and loungewear for women under the insense women brand; beauty products under the ARCELIA brand; fashion products under the Haute curry brand; color-first beauty products under the Joyology Beauty brand; and watches, leather handbags, high-fashion footwear, sunglasses, jewelry, and luggage. In addition, the company offers home décor, premium furnishing, and lifestyle products through HomeStop concept stores. Further, it is involved in the distribution of beauty brands; and retailing of various consumer products through an online channel. Shoppers Stop Limited was founded in 1991 and is based in Mumbai, India.
