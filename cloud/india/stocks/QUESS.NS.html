Company Overview

Quess Corp Limited provides staffing and workforce solutions in India and internationally. The company operates through four segments: General Staffing, Professional Staffing, Overseas Staffing, and Digital Platforms. It offers general staffing, including tailor-made solutions for diverse staffing requirements and expert recruitment solutions. The company also provides professional staffing, such as digital workplace solutions, cloud and datacenter services, and managed network services; staff augmentation services, such as contract hiring services; hire, train, and deploy (HTD) programs; interview as a service (IAAS); workforce optimization; international skill mobility and turnkey workforce solutions; and global capability centre services across areas, such as cloud, cybersecurity, and enterprise platforms, as well as operates offshore development centers for flexible staffing and optimizes IT workforce management through its managed capacity services. In addition, it offers digital platforms, including Hamara Academy, an upskilling platform; Hamara Jobs, a proprietary blue-collar auto-hiring technology platform; Hamara HR, a workforce management tool; Hamara Benefits, an employee benefit and financial wellness platform; and Taskmo, a gig discovery platform that enables enterprises scale business operations through digital hiring solutions, as well as digital and hyperlocal solutions for financial products, e-commerce, q-commerce, food tech, grocery, mobility, rental, pharmacy, retail, and other industries through its network of gig workers. Further, it provides overseas staffing solution that connects businesses with skilled talent enabling workforce deployment across industries through regional expertise and cross-border mobility. The company was formerly known as Ikya Human Capital Solutions Limited and changed its name to Quess Corp Limited in January 2015. Quess Corp Limited was incorporated in 2007 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, India.
