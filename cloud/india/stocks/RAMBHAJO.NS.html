Company Overview

Advit Jewels Limited designs, manufactures, and sells traditional and contemporary handcrafted fine jewelry. It offers necklaces, earrings, rings, bangles, and customized jewelry pieces primarily with gold, diamonds, and colored stones. The company operates under the brand name of Rambhajo. It sells its products through the B2B model, the B2C model, dealers, showrooms, and jewelry retailers. The company was founded in 1921 and is based in Jaipur, India.
