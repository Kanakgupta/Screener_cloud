Alkyl Amines Chemicals Limited manufactures and supplies amines, amine derivatives, and other specialty chemicals in India and internationally. The company offers aliphatic amines, such as methylamines, ethylamines, isopropylamines, n-propylamines, n-butylamines, ethylhexylamines, furfurylamines, and cyclohexylamines; and amine derivatives, including dimethylamine hydrochloride, diethylamine hydrochloride, triethylamine hydrochloride, 3-ethoxy propylamine, 3-(methylaminopropyl) amine, dimethylaminopropylamine, 3-(2-ethylhexyloxy) propylamine, n-methylbenzylamine, diisopropyl ethylamine, diethylhydroxylamine, diethyl toluamide, and diethylphenyl acetamide. The company serves pharmaceutical, agro-chemicals, water treatment chemicals, rubber chemicals, and other industries. Alkyl Amines Chemicals Limited was incorporated in 1979 and is headquartered in Navi Mumbai, India.
