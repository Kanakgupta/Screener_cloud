Company Overview

Shalibhadra Finance Limited operates as an asset finance company in India. The company offers new and used two-wheeler, used three/four-wheeler, auto rickshaw, cars, and consumer durable loans. It serves the salaried class, agriculturist, and self-employed customers in the rural, semi-urban, and other under banked areas of Gujarat, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Rajasthan. Shalibhadra Finance Limited was incorporated in 1992 and is based in Mumbai, India.
