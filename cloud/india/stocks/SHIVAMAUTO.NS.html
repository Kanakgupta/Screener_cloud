Company Overview

Shivam Autotech Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of auto transmission components for original equipment manufacturers in India and internationally. The company offers transmission gears and shafts; spline shafts; plungers; pinions; industrial and wind turbine gears; and power tool, power train, alternator, starter motor, aerospace, home appliances, electronic power steering, diesel injection system, e-rickshaw axle, EPS, magneto, steering, and various precision engineering components, as well as aluminum forging parts. Itis involved in aerospace, industrial, and renewable energy; and provision of power and construction tools. The company provides auto components for two-wheeler, three-wheeler, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, and tractors sectors. The company was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Gurugram, India. Shivam Autotech Limited is a subsidiary of Dayanand Munjal Investments Private Limited.
