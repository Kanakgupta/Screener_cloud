Company Overview

Akshar Spintex Limited engages in the spinning, manufacture, and sale of cotton yarns in India. The company offers natural cotton, organic and BCI certified cotton, viscose, modal, and excel fibres. It also produces carded, semi combed, and combed cotton yarns; and slub, core spun, TFO, eli twist, fancy, melange, blended, BCI certified, and organic yarns. Akshar Spintex Limited was incorporated in 2013 and is based in Rajkot, India.
