Company Overview

Tiger Logistics (India) Limited provides inbound and outbound logistics solutions in India and internationally. The company offers air freight, ocean freight, project and defense, domestic Freight, customs clearance, cold chain logistics, and supply chain financing services. It also operates through FreightJar, a digital freight booking and management platform. Tiger Logistics (India) Limited was incorporated in 2000 and is based in New Delhi, India.
