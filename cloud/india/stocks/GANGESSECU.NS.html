Company Overview

Ganges Securities Limited, through its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacture and sale of tea and other related agricultural produce in India. It is also involved in tea processing activities; and invests and deals in securities and in immovable properties. The company was incorporated in 2015 and is based in Kolkata, India.
