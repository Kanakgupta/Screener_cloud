Company Overview

Zensar Technologies Limited, a digital solutions and technology services company, provides technology consulting and system integration services in India, the Americas, Europe, Africa, and internationally. It operates through Digital and Application Services, and Cloud Infrastructure Services segments. The company offers- AI/ML, data annotation, data engineering, visualization and analytics, Al foundry intelligence, physical Al foundry, accelerated generative Al, automation, responsible Al for Gen Al, data Al and offering, and Google GenAl products, as well as engineering and platforms, digital experience platforms, and cloud transformation and operations. It also provides data engineering and analytics services comprising accelerated gen AI, Google gen AI, data engineering, AI and machine learning, automation, and visualization and analytics; and application management, quality engineering, enterprise change management, Oracle, salesforce, SAP, and veeva services. In addition, the company offers cloud infrastructure and security solutions, such as cloud, hybrid infrastructure, digital workplace, enterprise system management, and security; AI solutions; and brand strategy and design, consulting, and marketing and insights solutions. It serves manufacturing, retail, media, technology and telecom, consumer service, banking and financial service, insurance, healthcare and life sciences, public sector, and utilities industries. Zensar Technologies Limited was formerly known as Fujitsu ICIM Limited and changed its name to Zensar Technologies Limited in August 2001. Zensar Technologies Limited was incorporated in 1963 and is headquartered in Pune, India.
