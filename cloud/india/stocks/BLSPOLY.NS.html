Company Overview

BLS Polymers Limited manufactures polymer compounds for the wire and cable and industrial pipeline coating industries. The company operates through two segments: Polymer and Trading. The company manufactures polyethylene compounds, including high-density polyethylene, medium-density polyethylene, low-density polyethylene, and linear low-density polyethylene variants, used for insulation, jacketing, and sheathing of power, telecom, and optical fiber cables. It also manufactures cross-linked polyethylene compounds, silane-grafted, for insulation of low-tension and high-tension power cables, including aerial bunched cables. Additionally, the company manufactures halogen-free flame retardant compounds for insulation and jacketing of cables, as well as polyvinyl chloride compounds for insulation and sheathing of power, data, signaling, automotive, and house wiring cables. It also offers cable filling compounds for moisture resistance and insulation in telecom cables, along with specialty additive, black, and white masterbatches for polymer products. For industrial pipeline coating applications, the company manufactures high-density polyethylene top coat compounds for the outer layer of three-layer polyethylene pipeline coating systems used on oil, gas, and water distribution pipelines, and polypropylene top coat compounds for three-layer polypropylene pipeline coating applications. It also manufactures adhesive compounds, which serve as intermediate layer adhesives for bonding between the fusion bonded epoxy base layer and the top coat layer in three-layer pipeline coating systems. Additionally, the company trades polymers and operates as a stockist and distributor of polymer products. BLS Polymers Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is based in New Delhi, India.
