Company Overview

Suraj Limited manufactures and sells stainless steel seamless pipes, tubes, U tubes, flanges, and fittings in India. It offers seamless instrumentation tubes, seamless heat exchanger tubes, seamless heat exchanger U tubes, and seamless buttweld fittings, as well as tubes, electro polished tubes, flanges and stub ends, carbon steel tubes, and forged rings. The company serves pharmaceuticals, dyes and pigments, oil, gas, refinery, etc. industries. It exports its products to approximately 70 countries. Suraj Limited was founded in 1960 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
