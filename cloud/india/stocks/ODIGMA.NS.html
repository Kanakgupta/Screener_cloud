Company Overview

Odigma Consultancy Solutions Limited operates as a digital marketing agency and domain registry service provider in India. It design, development, and optimisation of websites and digital platforms; and offers email marketing, social media, online reputation management, video marketing, programmatic advertising, search engine marketing and optimization, and content marketing services. The company was founded in 2010 and is based in Bengaluru, India.
