Company Overview

Pradeep Metals Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of closed die steel forgings and processing activities. The company operates through Power Generation and Closed Die Forging and Processing segments. It offers forged flanges, valves, manifolds, general engineering, and other material grade products. The company also generates power from wind turbine generator and solar power generating system. It has operations in India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Singapore, Sweden, Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, Mexico, New Zealand, and Argentina. Pradeep Metals Limited was incorporated in 1982 and is based in Navi Mumbai, India.
