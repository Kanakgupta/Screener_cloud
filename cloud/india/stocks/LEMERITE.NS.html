Company Overview

Le Merite Exports Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacturing, purchase, and sale of textile in India and internationally. It offers cotton, cotton blended, and synthetic yarns; greige and finished fabrics; and weaving job units, such as machinery details. The company was incorporated in 2003 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
