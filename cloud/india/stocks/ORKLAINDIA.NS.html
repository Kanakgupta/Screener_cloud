Company Overview

Orkla India Limited operates as a food company, manufactures and sells spices and convenience foods in India and internationally. It offers sambar, chicken, puliogare, rasam, and meat blended spice masala; and chilli, kashmiri chilli, turmeric, coriander, and cumin pure spices. The company also provides ready-to-cook, such as breakfast, sweet, and badam milk beverage mixes; ready-to-eat, includes paneer butter masala, dal makhani, and chana masala, as well as various rice dishes such as veg pulao and tomato rice; vermicelli and macaroni; badam drink and coffee beverages, pickles, ginger garlic paste, malabar parotas, cooking aids, pulses, rice, coconut milk, desserts, and other confectionery items. It sells its products through e-commerce websites and commerce platform under the MTR, Eastern, Rasoi Magic, Wok N Roll, and Laban brand names. The company was formerly known as MTR Foods Private Limited. The company was founded in 1924 and is based in Bengaluru, India. Orkla India Limited is a subsidiary of Orkla Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd.
