Company Overview

TGB Banquets and Hotels Limited provides restaurant, banquet, and hotel services in India. The company operates The Grand Bhagwati hotel located in Ahmedabad. It also engages in the operation of restaurants under the Café Piano and Perq; and meetings and convention centers. In addition, the company offers catering and event services. The company was formerly known as Bhagwati Banquets and Hotels Limited and changed its name to TGB Banquets and Hotels Limited in April 2013. TGB Banquets and Hotels Limited was founded in 1989 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
