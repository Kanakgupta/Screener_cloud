Company Overview

Gujarat Raffia Industries Limited manufactures and sells HDPE tarpaulin products in India and internationally. The company offers PE tarpaulins, plastic and ground sheeting, geomembranes, tents, shelters, pond and canal linings, sand bags, hay trap, shade net, lumber wraps, rain coats, HDPE and PP woven bags, azolla beds, wagon and fumigation cover, grow bags, weed mats, vermibed and ropes, etc. It also exports its products. The company was incorporated in 1984 and is based in Gandhinagar, India.
