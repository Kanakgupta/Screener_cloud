Company Overview

Paras Healthcare Limited provides tertiary and quaternary healthcare services. The company provides cardiac sciences, neurosciences, oncology sciences, gastro sciences, orthopedics and sports injury, and renal sciences services. It offers services, such as cardio thoracic vascular surgery and cardiology; medical, radiation, surgical, and hemato oncology, as well as nuclear medicine and bone marrow transplant; and neuroscience, neuro intervention, neurology, and spine surgery. The company also provides gastrointestinal surgery, gastroenterology, and liver transplant; nephrology, urology, and kidney transplant; general and minimal access surgery, obstetrics, gynecology, paediatrics, internal medicine, pulmonology, plastic and reconstructive surgery, and endocrinology as well as ear, nose, throat, neck and head surgery. Paras Healthcare Limited was incorporated in 1987 and is based in Gurugram, India.
