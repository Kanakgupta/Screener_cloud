Company Overview

Rishabh Instruments Limited engages in the design, development, and manufacturing of global energy efficiency solutions in electrical automation, measurement, and industrial technology in Asia, Europe, and internationally. The company offers electrical automation devices, including transducers, isolators, and temperature controllers; metering, control, and protection devices, such as analogue panel meters, rotary cam switches, current transformers, shunts, digital panel meters, multi-function meters, power quality meters, power factor controllers, protection relays, and power supplies; portable test and measuring instruments, including digital multimeters, digital clamp meters, insulation testers, earth testers, and other testing and measurement solutions. It also offers solar string inverters, SCADA software, industrial automation, test and measurement solutions, and aluminum die casting. In addition, it provides analog panel meters include a variety of electrical and measuring instruments such as battery charger, cam switches, clamp meters, current transformers, digital multimeter, digital panel meter, earth testers, insulation testers, isolators, low resistance meter, lumel products, mid din-rail ac energy meters, multifunction meters, power factor controller, power quality, power supply, product accessories, protector relays, shunts, solar inverters, transducers, and VA instruments products. It serves Manufacturers and traders of electrical Instruments. The company was formerly known as Rishabh Instruments Private Limited and changed its name to Rishabh Instruments Limited in September 2022. Rishabh Instruments Limited was incorporated in 1982 and is headquartered in Nashik, India.
