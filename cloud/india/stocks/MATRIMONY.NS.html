Company Overview

Matrimony.com Limited, a consumer internet company, provides online matchmaking services on internet and mobile platforms in India and internationally. It operates through two segments: Matchmaking Services and Marriage Services. The company offers online matchmaking services, including EliteMatrimony.com, BharatMatrimony.com, CommunityMatrimony.com, and the Jodi application, as well as wedding services comprising WeddingBazaar.com, Mandap.com, and HappyMarriages.com. It also operates regional matrimony sites, such as BharatMatrimony.com, BengaliMatrimony.com, AssameseMatrimony.com, BihariMatrimony.com, GujaratiMatrimony.com, HindiMatrimony.com, KannadaMatrimony.com, KeralaMatrimony.com, MarathiMatrimony.com, MarwadiMatrimony.com, OriyaMatrimony.com, ParsiMatrimony.com, PunjabiMatrimony.com, RajasthaniMatrinomy.com, SindhiMatrimony.com, TamilMatrimony.com, TeluguMatrimony.com, and UrduMatrimony.com; religion based matrimonial sites, which consists of MuslimMatrimony.com, ChristianMatrimony.com, SikhMatrimony.com, JainMatrimony.com, and BuddhistMatrimony.com; and DefenceMatrimony.com, DivorceeMatrimony.com, EliteMatrimony.com, and ManglikMatrimony.com. Matrimony.com Limited was founded in 1997 and is based in Chennai, India.
