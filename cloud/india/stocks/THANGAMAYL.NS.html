About

Thangamayil Jewellery Limited operates a chain of retail jewelry stores in India. The company offers gold, diamond, silver, precious stones, and platinum jewelries, as well as silver articles and gold coins. It also involved in the manufacture and sale of jewelry, and other accessories and products. In addition, the company offers gold saving schemes. It provides its products through online. Thangamayil Jewellery Limited was founded in 1947 and is headquartered in Madurai, India.

Highlights
  • Retail Network Expansion: Thangamayil Jewellery has been consistently expanding its retail footprint, primarily in Tamil Nadu, India, with a focus on tier 2 and tier 3 cities.
  • Product Diversification: While primarily focused on gold, the company has been increasing its offerings in diamond, silver, and platinum jewellery to cater to a broader customer base.
  • Gold Saving Schemes: The company offers various gold saving schemes, which help in customer retention and provide a steady stream of advance payments for future purchases.
  • Online Presence: Thangamayil Jewellery has an online presence, allowing customers to browse and purchase products digitally, complementing its physical store network.
Strategic Focus

Thangamayil Jewellery's strategy is centered on expanding its retail network within South India, particularly in Tamil Nadu, to capture a larger share of the organized jewellery market. The company aims to enhance its product portfolio with a focus on design innovation and customer-centric gold saving schemes, while also leveraging its online platform for broader reach.
