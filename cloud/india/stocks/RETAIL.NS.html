Company Overview

JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Limited operates a chain of retail stores in India. It operates through Location-Based Retailing, Format-Based Operations, and Product & Brand Segments. The company operates flagship stores, compact and convenience stores, and pop-up and experience stores, as well as high-street outlets, transit hubs, and shopping malls and commercial complexes. It offers lifestyle and fashion, personal care and wellness, partnership, franchise brands, and food and beverages. The company was formerly known as JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Private Limited. JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Limited was incorporated in 2007 and is based in New Delhi, India.
