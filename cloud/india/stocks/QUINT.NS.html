Company Overview

Quint Digital Limited operates as a digital and media-tech/AI focused company in India and internationally. It also operates Quintype, a digital newsroom platform that enables publishers to create, distribute, monetise their content; The Quint, a platform for news and views; Quint Hindi, a cutting-edge journalism in hindi; The News Minute, an independent news platform focus on five southern states; and Youth Ki Awaaz, a crowdsourced platform for young people to write and share stories. In addition, the company engages in the operation of websites through web, digital or mobile media, which include information related to current affairs, lifestyle, entertainment, etc. The company was formerly known as Quint Digital Media Limited and changed its name to Quint Digital Limited in October 2023. Quint Digital Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is headquartered in New Delhi, India.
