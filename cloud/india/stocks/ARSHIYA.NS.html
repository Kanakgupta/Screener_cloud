Company Overview

Arshiya Limited provides integrated supply chain and logistics infrastructure solutions in India. The company operates in two segments, Free Trade Warehousing Zones (FTWZ)/Special Economic Zone (SEZ) and Domestic Warehousing. It develops and operates FTWZs that include integrated logistics infrastructure comprising storage areas, material handling equipment, container yards, inland container depot, customs offices, and commercial complex. The company also offers third-party logistics and other value optimization services, such as handling and transportation, packaging, consolidation, palletization, labelling, kitting, bagging, bottling, cutting-slitting, survey, quality assurance, refurbishment, repairs and maintenance, washing, etc. In addition, it provides infrastructure and freight services. The company was formerly known as Arshiya International Limited and changed its name to Arshiya Limited in September 2013. Arshiya Limited was incorporated in 1981 and is based in Mumbai, India.
