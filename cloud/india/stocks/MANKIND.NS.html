Mankind Pharma Limited MANKIND NIFTY_MIDNSEPHARMA

Healthcare · Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic · India
https://www.mankindpharma.com

Mankind Pharma Limited develops, manufactures, and markets pharmaceutical formulations and consumer healthcare products in India and internationally. The company develops pharmaceuticals for acute and chronic therapeutics in the areas of anti-infective, cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, gynaecology, anti-diabetic, dermatology, pain/analgesics, neuro/CNS, vitamins/minerals/nutrients, respiratory diseases, etc. It also provides consumer healthcare products, such as condoms, pregnancy detection kits, oral contraceptives, antacid powders, vitamin and mineral supplements, and anti-acne preparations. In addition, the company engages in the trading and exporting of pharmaceutical and health care products; manufacture of packing materials, ayurvedic products, and consumer goods; real estate, leasing, and hospitality businesses; trading of agricultural products; and provision of IT services. It offers its products under the Manforce, Moxi Kind-Cv, AmLokind-At, Unwanted-Kit, Prega News, Dydroboon, Gudcef, Candi Force, Glimestar-M, Telmikind-Am, Nurokind-Gold, Telmikind-H, Nurokind-Lc, Telmikind, Vomikind, Cefakind, Nurokind Plus-Rf, Gudcef-Cv, Monticope, and Asthakind-Dx brand names. Mankind Pharma Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is based in New Delhi, India.

Highlights
  • Manufacturing Facilities: Operates 30 manufacturing units, including 22 in Paonta Sahib (HP), 2 in Haridwar (UK), and 2 in Udaipur & Behror (Rajasthan).
  • PAN India Presence: 16,000+ field force, 13,000+ stockists, 75 CFAs, reaching 5,00,000+ doctors and a vast chemist & pharmacy network across India.
  • Exports: Exports to USA (major market), Sri Lanka, Nepal, Chile, Kenya and others.
  • Exports Portfolio: 42 products in US portfolio; 1 new product launched in Q3FY25 and 4 in 9MFY25.
  • R&D Initiatives: 6 R&D centres; spent INR 233 Cr (~2.1% of FY24 revenue). Focus on biotechnology, sustainability and a GPR119 agonist NCE for obesity/diabetes - Phase 1 complete, Phase 2 planned.
  • BSV Acquisition: Acquired the branded generic women's health portfolio from subsidiary Bharat Serums & Vaccines (slump sale, INR 797 Cr, two installments).
Strategic Focus

Consolidate market share via deeper penetration in high-value CVM and chronic therapies, supported by volume-led growth and brand scaling. Expand into super-specialty and complex portfolios via acquisitions and in-licensing. Strengthen presence in metros and Tier-I cities and grow consumer healthcare. Continued investment in digital platforms, AI/ML productivity tools, and R&D for high-entry-barrier pipeline.
