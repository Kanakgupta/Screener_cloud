Company Overview

Bharat Seats Limited manufactures and sells seating systems and moulded carpets and extruded components for the automotive and surface transport industries in India. It offers seats for four-wheeler, two-wheeler, and railway chair cars; carpets; roof and windshield modeling products; and two-wheeler frame parts. The company also offers NVH components like floor carpet, silencer dash inner, silencer dash outer, motor cover under, fender lining, wheel housing cover for passenger cars; and design & development of rear doors for passenger cars. The company was incorporated in 1986 and is based in Gurugram, India.
