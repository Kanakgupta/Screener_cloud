Company Overview

Shraddha Prime Projects Limited engages in the real estate development business in India. It engages in developing, leasing, constructing, and redeveloping various residential and commercial projects. The company was formerly known as Towa Sokki Limited and changed its name to Shraddha Prime Projects Limited in July 2021. Shraddha Prime Projects Limited was incorporated in 1993 and is based in Mumbai, India.
