Himatsingka Seide Limited HIMATSEIDE NSE

Consumer Cyclical · Textile Manufacturing · India
https://www.himatsingka.com

Himatsingka Seide Limited designs, develops, manufactures, distributes, and retails textile products in North America, India, the Asia Pacific, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and internationally. The company offers bedding, bath, yarn and fiber, and drapery and upholstery solutions. It offers its products under the Disney, Himeya, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, Pimacott, Organicott, Gizacott, Homegrown Cotton, and Bellora brand names. Himatsingka Seide Limited was incorporated in 1985 and is based in Bengaluru, India.
