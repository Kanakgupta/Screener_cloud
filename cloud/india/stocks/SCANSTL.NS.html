Company Overview

Scan Steels Limited manufactures and sells steel products in India. The company offers thermo mechanical treatment rods under the SHRISHTII TMT brand name; sponge iron, MS billets, and ingots; and PVC coated sheets under the SHRISHTII ROOFING brand name. It also provides structures, such as flats, angles, square bars, channels, and round-plain rods; and fly ash bricks. Scan Steels Limited is based in Bhubaneswar, India.
