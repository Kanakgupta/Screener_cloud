Company Overview

Oriental Aromatics Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells fragrances, flavours, specialty aroma ingredients, and camphor and terpene chemicals in India. The company offers flavours and fragrances for use in beverages, oral hygiene, sweets, instant foods, dairy products, incense sticks, fine fragrances, personal care, and home care; specialty aroma ingredient for fragrance compounds, flavour formulations, personal care, home care, fine fragrances, cosmetics, and other aroma-led consumer applications; and camphor and terpene chemicals for puja and devotional, household applications, pain-relief products, topical formulations, pharmaceutical preparations, and flavours. It also exports its products to Egypt, Kenya, Morocco, South Africa, Zambia, Bangladesh, China, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Israel, Japan, Lebanon, Malaysia, Nepal, Oman, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates, Vietnam, Yemen, Austria, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Poland, Spain, Switzerland, Turkey, the United Kingdom, Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Canada, Mexico, and the United States. The company serves various customers, including FMCG, personal care, home care, food, pharma, pain management, and devotional product categories. The company was formerly known as Camphor & Allied Products Limited and changed its name to Oriental Aromatics Limited in February 2018. Oriental Aromatics Limited was founded in 1955 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
