Utilities · Utilities - Independent Power Producers · India

RattanIndia Power Limited, together with its subsidiary, Poena Power Development Limited, engages in power generation, distribution, trading and transmission, and other ancillary and incidental activities in India. It generates electricity through coal-based thermal power plants with an installed capacity of 2,700 MW located in Amravati and Nasik cities. The company also trades electricity in the open market. RattanIndia Power Limited was incorporated in 2007 and is based in New Delhi, India.
