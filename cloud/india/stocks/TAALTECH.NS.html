Company Overview

TAAL Tech Limited provides engineering design services in India and internationally. The company operates in two segments: Air Charter and Engineering Design Service. It offers smart services, robotic process automation, cloud services and DevOps, machine learning and big data analytics, industrial Internet of Things, software product engineering and UI/UX, and remote device management for manufacturing, and infusion support services. The company serves to architecture, engineering, and construction-BIM services, plant engineering, industrial engineering and heavy equipment, transportation, aerospace and defense, and consumer electronic industries. The company was formerly known as TAAL Enterprises Limited and changed its name to TAAL Tech Limited in November 2025. The company was incorporated in 2014 and is based in Bengaluru, India. TAAL Tech Limited is a subsidiary of Vishkul Enterprises Private Limited.
