Highlights
  • Brand Portfolio: Operates hotels and cruisers under the luxury 'Oberoi' and upscale 'Trident' brand names.
  • Diversified Services: Beyond hotels, also operates Oberoi Flight Services, Oberoi Airport Services, restaurants, lounges, and provides air charter and car rental services.
  • Strategic Partnership: Has a long-standing strategic alliance with Mandarin Oriental Hotel Group for global sales and marketing.
  • Property Ownership: Engages in renting of investment properties, complementing its core hospitality operations.
Strategic Focus

EIH Limited is focused on expanding its luxury and upscale hotel portfolio, both domestically and internationally, while enhancing its service offerings in flight catering and airport services. The company aims to leverage its strong brand reputation to capture growth in the premium hospitality sector and optimize returns from its existing asset base.
