Company Overview

Suzlon Energy Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells wind turbine generators and related components in India and internationally. It offers products, such as on-shore wind turbine generators with capacities ranging from 2 MW to 6 MW, and classic fleet turbines; and services, including end-to-end project development through its RE DevCo business, energy storage solutions, and renewable energy asset management. It operates through the WTG, Foundry & Forging, O&M services, and Others segments. The company also provides operation and maintenance services for wind turbine generators, as well as project execution, procurement, marketing, and shared service center-related services. In addition, it is involved in the sale/sub-lease of land; manufacture and sale of forging rings and casting articles, as well as power generation and evacuation infrastructure facilities. The company was incorporated in 1995 and is headquartered in Pune, India.
