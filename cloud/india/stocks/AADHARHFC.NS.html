Company Overview

Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, a housing finance company, engages in the housing finance business in India. The company offers loans for residential property; plot purchase and construction; home improvement and extension loans; commercial property construction and acquisition; and loans against residential properties. It serves individuals, companies, corporations, societies, and association of persons. The company was formerly known as DHFL Vysya Housing Finance Limited and changed its name to Aadhar Housing Finance Limited in August 2017. Aadhar Housing Finance Limited was incorporated in 1990 and is based in Mumbai, India. Aadhar Housing Finance Limited is a subsidiary of Bcp Asia Ii Holdco Vii Pte. Ltd.
