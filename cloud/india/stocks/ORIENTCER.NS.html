Company Overview

Orient Ceratech Limited engages in the producing and trading of aluminum refractories and monolithic products in India. The company operates through two segments: Alumina Refactories & Monolithics Products & Bauxite Ores; and Power Generation. It offers various aluminous products, including White Fused Alumina, Dense White Fused Alumina, Brown Fused Alumina, White Fused Mullite, Fused Zirconia Mullite, Calcined Bauxite, Chamotte, and Mullite. In addition, the company provides conventional and low cement, ultra-low cement, refractory mortars, special, insulating, chrome base, zircon base, and delta castables, as well as neutral ramming mass (NRM), an alumina-based solution; ceramic and silica proppants; foundry sands and coatings; and synthetic aggregates. Further, it generates electricity through thermal power plant, furnace oil-based power plant, and windmills for state power distribution companies, as well as operates mines for bauxite ores. It also exports its products. The company was formerly known as Orient Abrasives Limited and changed its name to Orient Ceratech Limited in May 2023. Orient Ceratech Limited was incorporated in 1971 and is based in Mumbai, India.
