Company Overview

Berger Paints India Limited manufactures and sells paints for home, professional, and industrial users in India and internationally. The company offers interior wall coatings, including designer finishes, interior emulsions, and ceiling paints; exterior wall coatings comprising exterior emulsions and textures, and roof and floor coatings; specialty industrial coatings; putty and cement paints; solvent, interior, exterior, metal, and wood primers; waterproofing solutions; wood finishes; enamels; aerosol spray paints, and DIY kits and stickers. It also provides automotive, fireproof, protective, floor, and powder coatings; road markings; construction chemicals, flooring compounds, bitumen and coal tar-based products, sealants and adhesives, and anti-corrosive coatings; lacquers; and various sanitization products. In addition, the company offers acrylic, silicate, and mineral plasters; thermal insulation adhesives; mortars; ready to use coat, wood effect, ribbed effect, concrete effect, and 3D effect; gel adhesives; and coil coating solutions. The company was formerly known as British Paints (India) Limited and changed its name to Berger Paints India Limited in December 1983. The company was incorporated in 1923 and is based in Kolkata, India. Berger Paints India Limited is a subsidiary of U. K. Paints India Private Limited.
