Company Overview

Diligent Media Corporation Limited engages in the production, curation, creation, conversion, procurement, buying, selling, and distribution of various forms of multimedia content through digital media in India and internationally. The company's multimedia content includes digital news, videos, documentaries, and photo series. It also provides content through DNA Syndication. Diligent Media Corporation Limited was incorporated in 2005 and is based in Noida, India.
