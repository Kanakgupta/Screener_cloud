Company Overview

Noida Toll Bridge Company Limited engages in the development, establishment, construction, operation, and maintenance of Delhi Noida toll bridge on a build-own-operate-transfer basis in India. It also provides space rentals for advertisements and offices. Noida Toll Bridge Company Limited was founded in 1976 and is based in Noida, India.
