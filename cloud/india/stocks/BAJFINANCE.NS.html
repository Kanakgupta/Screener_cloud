AI Exposure AI NBFC / AI-Driven Retail Credit
Core Focus

Bajaj Finance is the most AI-mature NBFC in India. Uses ML for alternative credit scoring (behaviour-based, e-commerce purchase data), instant loan approval (<2 minutes for existing customers), AI-powered collections optimisation, and dynamic pricing of EMI products. 'BFL AI Score' is a proprietary model for thin-file customers.

Key Initiatives
  • AI-first underwriting at scale Bajaj Finance runs one of India's most data-rich consumer-lending engines, using ML for instant credit decisions, risk-based pricing and fraud control across tens of millions of customers.
  • Bajaj app & AI cross-sell The consumer app uses AI-driven personalisation to cross-sell EMI cards, loans and insurance, deepening wallet share and lowering acquisition cost.
  • AI in collections & early-warning Predictive models flag stress early and optimise collections, a key driver of the company's best-in-class asset quality.
Why Investors Should Care

  • Distribution + data network effects are hard for new fintechs to replicate.
  • Premium P/E ~30 reflects quality; growth must stay strong to sustain it.
