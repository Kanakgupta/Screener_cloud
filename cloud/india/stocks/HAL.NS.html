Company Overview

Hindustan Aeronautics Limited engages in the design, development, manufacture, repair, overhaul, upgrade, and servicing of aircraft, helicopters, aero-engines, avionics, accessories, and aerospace structures in India and internationally. It serves national and international defense customers, as well as civil customers. Hindustan Aeronautics Limited was incorporated in 1963 and is based in Bengaluru, India.
