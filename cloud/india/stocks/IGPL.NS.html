Company Overview

I G Petrochemicals Limited manufactures and sells chemical products in India and internationally. The company offers phthalic anhydride, maleic anhydride, benzoic acid, and diethyl phthalate. Its products are used in plasticizers, paints and coatings, construction and infrastructure, automotive, FRP/composites, food and beverage, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics and fragrances, agrochemicals, electrical and electronics, textiles, and specialty chemicals industries. The company was incorporated in 1988 and is based in Mumbai, India.
