Company Overview

Hindustan Composites Limited develops, manufactures, and markets fibre-based friction materials in India. It operates through Composite Products and Investment segments. The company offers friction materials, including automotive brake linings, automotive and industrial roll linings, automotive clutch facings and ceramic buttons, automotive disc brake pads, railway brake blocks, and friction sheets. It also provides industrial thrust bearing and insulation products comprising millboard sheets and discs, compestos, and compestos rings. In addition, the company engages in investment activities. It serves various industries, such as automotive, railways, engineering, mining, aerospace, steel, chemical, and oil exploration, etc. Hindustan Composites Limited was incorporated in 1964 and is based in Mumbai, India.
