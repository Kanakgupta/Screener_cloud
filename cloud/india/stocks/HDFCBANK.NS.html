Core Focus

HDFC Bank's AI strategy centres on EVA (intelligent virtual assistant with 500M+ interactions), AI-powered credit risk models for retail/SME lending, and fraud detection (AI flags 95%+ of fraud transactions). SmartHub Vyapar (merchant app) uses AI for cash-flow-based lending. Also using AI for ATM predictive maintenance and branch footfall optimization.

Key Initiatives
  • AI in underwriting, risk & fraud HDFC Bank applies ML across credit scoring, fraud detection and early-warning systems on India's largest private-bank balance sheet.

  • Operations & process automation AI/RPA at scale across service, collections and back-office processes drives efficiency.
