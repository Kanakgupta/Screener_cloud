Company Overview

WPIL Limited, together with its subsidiaries, designs, develops, manufactures, erects, commissions, and services pumps and pumping systems in India and internationally. It offers mixed and axial flow, as well as turbine; split case, multistage, end suction, and non clog; deepwell, drainage, sewage, and dewatering; concrete and metallic volutes, as well as pump turbine; vertical, horizontal, and system packages fire fighting solutions; and floating, gate, and axial flow submersible pumps. The company's products are used in water, irrigation, municipal, and energy applications. It exports its products. The company was incorporated in 1952 and is based in Kolkata, India.
