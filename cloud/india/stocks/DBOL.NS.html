Company Overview

Dhampur Bio Organics Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells sugar in India and internationally. The company operates through three segments: Sugar; Biofuels and Spirits; and Country Liquor. It offers sulphurless sugar and sugar byproducts under the Dhampure brand name; ethanol and biogenic carbon dioxide; spirit and liquor; extra neutral alcohol; bagasse; and organic fertilizer. The company also engages in the cogeneration and sale of power. It exports its products. The company was incorporated in 2020 and is based in New Delhi, India.
