Company Overview

D.P. Wires Limited manufactures and supplies steel wires, plastic pipes, and plastic films for oil and gas, power, environment, civil, energy, automobile, infrastructure, and other industries in India and internationally. It operates through Wire Division, Plastic Division, Wind Mill Division, and Trading Division segments. The company offers LRPC strand wires, induction tempered wires, geomembrane sheets, and spring steel wires, as well as specialized steel wires. It also provides PE films, pond lining films, HDPE films, cap covers, wires, and others. In addition, the company is involved in the wind power generation and trading businesses. D.P. Wires Limited was founded in 1971 and is headquartered in Ratlam, India.
