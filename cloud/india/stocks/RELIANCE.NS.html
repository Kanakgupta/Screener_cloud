Conglomerate / AI Infrastructure

Reliance Jio is building India's largest AI cloud infrastructure — JioCloud with GPU clusters (10,000+ NVIDIA H100 GPUs, announced FY25). Jio AI-Cloud aims to offer sovereign AI compute to Indian enterprises and government at scale. Also applying AI across retail (personalisation), O2C (refinery optimization) and media (content recommendations on JioCinema).

AI Investment₹75,000 Cr+ total capex in FY25, significant portion toward Jio 5G and AI data centres. ₹10,000+ Cr in AI data centre buildout under Jio Platforms. Signed MoU with NVIDIA for AI infrastructure.
AI RevenueJio Platforms revenue ~₹1.07 lakh Cr in FY25 (broadband + enterprise). AI-specific revenue not yet separately disclosed; enterprise cloud + AI services segment nascent but growing. JioCloud enterprise sign-ups growing.
ROI TodayAI ROI indirect — Jio network AI reducing capex per GB and improving spectrum efficiency. Retail AI boosting conversion rates. Direct monetisation of JioCloud AI services still early stage (FY25-26).
ROI ExpectedJio AI-Cloud positions Reliance as the 'picks and shovels' play for India's AI economy. Analysts see ₹20,000-30,000 Cr in AI-cloud revenue by FY28-29 if enterprise/government adoption accelerates. Transformative optionality in a regulated, high-barrier market.

Highlights
  • Diversified Conglomerate: Operates across Oil to Chemicals, Retail, Digital Services, and New Energy sectors, forming a highly diversified business portfolio.
  • Market Leadership: Jio Platforms is India's largest telecom operator by subscribers, and Reliance Retail is India's largest retailer by revenue and store count.
  • New Energy Push: Investing heavily in green energy initiatives, including solar, batteries, and hydrogen, with significant manufacturing capacities under development.
  • Integrated O2C Operations: Operates one of the world's largest and most complex integrated refining and petrochemical complexes at Jamnagar, India.
  • Strategic Investments: Continuously expanding through organic growth and strategic acquisitions in key sectors like retail, digital technology, and new energy.
Strategic Focus

Reliance Industries is focused on accelerating its transition into new energy businesses, while continuing to expand its leadership in India's retail and digital services sectors. The company aims to leverage its integrated ecosystem to drive sustainable growth and enhance shareholder value through strategic investments and operational excellence.
