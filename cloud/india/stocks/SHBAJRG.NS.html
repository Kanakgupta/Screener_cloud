Company Overview

Shri Bajrang Alliance Limited manufactures and sells structural steel products in India. The company operates in two segments, Steel and Agro. The company offers structural steel products, including angles, channels, joists, beams, rounds, flats, and H-beams. It also provides ready to eat frozen food products, as well as extracts oils and oleoresin essential oils. The company also exports its products. The company was formerly known as Shri Bajrang Alloys Limited and changed its name to Shri Bajrang Alliance Limited in December 2019. The company was incorporated in 1990 and is based in Raipur, India.
