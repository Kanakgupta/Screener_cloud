Company Overview

Mac Charles (India) Limited, together with its subsidiaries, is involved in the generation of electricity through wind turbine generators in India. It also constructs and leases commercial real estate properties. Mac Charles (India) Limited was incorporated in 1979 and is based in Bengaluru, India. The company operates as a subsidiary of Embassy Property Developments Private Limited.
