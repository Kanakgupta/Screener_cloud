Company Overview

IVP Limited engages in the manufacture and distributes chemicals in India. The company offers polyurethane systems for footwear; adhesives for flexible packaging industry; and foundry chemicals, coatings, composites, refractory, powder, and insulation resin products. IVP Limited was incorporated in 1929 and is based in Mumbai, India.
