Company Overview

MRF Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacture and sale of rubber products in India and internationally. The company provides tires for passenger cars, two wheelers, farms, trucks, OTR, SCV, LCV, pick up, three-wheeler, MCV, ICV, tubes, and flap products; tread rubber product; and manufactures specialty coatings. It is also involved in the paints and coats, sports goods, pretreads, and motorsports businesses; and heavy-duty truck/bus tyres, light truck, MUV/RCV/passenger cars, farm services, military services, fork-lift, off the road/earth movers, and MRF commercial tyres products. In addition, the company offers MRF tyres and services that provide buy tyres, tubeless repair, purest nitrogen filling, wheel alignment and balancing, and tire changing; MRF TireTok which offers robotic wheel aligner, vehicle safety test lane, diagnostic wheel balancer, semi-automatic tyre changer, A/C recovery and recharging unit, electronic headlight aligner, nitrogen generator membrane type, and special two-wheeler tire changer; MRF Tyredrome; MRF FASST that offers wheels removal and refitment, nitrogen inflation, and tire mounting and de-mounting services; and MRF MuscleZone, as well as provides training for light and heavy commercial vehicle driving. MRF Limited was founded in 1946 and is based in Chennai, India.
