Company Overview

Arrow Greentech Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacture and sale of green products in India and internationally. It operates in two segments, Green Products and High-Tech Products. The company offers water-soluble films, bio-compostable products, and other green products; and water-soluble laundry capsules, packaging, mold release, water transfer printing, water-soluble embroidery backing, and water-soluble seed tape films, as well as water-soluble laundry bags for agrochemicals and specialty chemicals packaging, construction fibers and chemicals packaging, textile processing, consumer goods and home care chemicals unit dose packaging, and medical and hygiene applications. It also provides BIOPLAST 105 and BIOPLAST GS 2189, a thermoplastic and transparent material that contains high portion of biologically sourced raw materials; and BIOPLAST GF 106/02, a thermoplastic material that contains natural potato starch. In addition, the company offers anti-counterfeit, intellectual property, and security based products, as well as pharmaceutical products; and deals in hightech products and services. Arrow Greentech Limited was formerly known as Arrow Coated Products Limited and changed its name to Arrow Greentech Limited in May 2016. The company was incorporated in 1992 and is based in Mumbai, India.
