Company Overview

Auri Grow India Limited manufactures and sells electronic cables and conductors in India. The company provides Aluminum Conductor Steel Reinforced, conductor wire, Aluminum Alloy Conductor, and AB cables. It also offers stay, and DPC wires, as well as XLP coated wires and conductors. The company was formerly known as Godha Cabcon & Insulation Limited and change its name to Auri Grow India Limited in October 2025. Auri Grow India Limited was founded in 1987 and is based in Indore, India.
