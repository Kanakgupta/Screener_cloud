Company Overview

HD Fire Protect Limited manufactures and supplies fire protection equipment and systems in India. The company offers sprinklers, flexible sprinkler drops, alarm valves, and accessories; deluge valves, deluge skids and pre-action systems; water spray nozzles; monitors and monitor nozzles; foam proportioning systems, foam equipment and devices, foam concentrates, and mobile equipment; systems valves and accessories, and handline nozzles; co2 fire suspension systems and inert gas fire suppression systems; and customized engineered systems, as well as spanning water suppression systems. Its products and solution are used in oil and gas, petrochemical and refining, power plants, commercial and residential buildings, hotels and airports, industrial and heavy engineering, data centres, ports and terminals, aviation, and other project industries. The company also exports. It operates in the Middle East, rest of Asia, South America, Europe, North America, Africa, and Oceania. The company was founded in 1990 and is headquartered in Thane, India.
