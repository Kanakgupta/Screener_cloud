Company Overview

Deepak Nitrite Limited manufactures, trades, and sells chemical intermediates in India and internationally. It operates through Advanced Intermediates and Phenolics segments. The company offers sodium nitrite, sodium nitrate, nitro toluidines, fuel additives, nitrosylsulfuric acid, xylidines, oximes, cumidines, speciality agrochemicals, optical brighteners, nitric acid, fluorinated compounds, DASDA, benzotrifluoride, cumene, phenol, acetone, isopropyl alcohol, and alpha methyl styrene. It also provides 2 MePPDA, 2,3-xylidine, 2,4-xylidine, 2,6-xylidine, 2-ethylhexyl nitrate, 3-NOX, 4-NOX; 2B, 4BBT, ABP, ABP X, and BMK deepwhite powders; 2BL, ABP, AS, BA, BHT HC, DSP, MST, SPF Z, SI, UBR, UP, and XBE deepwhite liquids; 4BB conc, 4BB conc T, DMA-X, DMA-X conc, and UFD slurry deepwhite products; IPA, MAHCL, mHBTF, MMDPA, MNT(3- nitrotoluene), MT(m-toluidine), ONT(2- nitrotoluene), OT(o-toluidine), PNT(4- nitrotoluene), and PT(p-toluidine); B+ (brown colour), FX (yellow colour), and GLP (yellow colour) protecto products; ortho and para cumidines; and TFMAP, as well as project management consultancy services and manufactures polycarbonate compounds. The company serves colourants, dyes, rubber, paper, agrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, personal care, water treatment, glass, explosives, textiles, laminate and plywood, automotive, construction, adhesives, and sanitisers industries. Deepak Nitrite Limited was incorporated in 1970 and is headquartered in Vadodara, India.
