Company Overview

Ultramarine & Pigments Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of pigments and surfactants in India. It operates through Chemicals and Allied Products, IT Enabled Services, and Wind Turbine Generator segments. The company offers pigments, such as ultramarine blue, violet, and pink; and bismuth vanadate; surfactants that include alpha olefin sulphonate, linear alkyl benzene, sulphonic acid LABSA, sodium lauryl, and sulphate; and specialty chemical, including medium chain triglyceride, benzalkonium chloride, CoCo amido propyl betaine, CoCo mono ethanol amide, CoCo diethanolamide, CoCo amide propy amine oxide, disodium laureth, sulfosuccinate, and lauryl amine oxide. It also provides various grades of detergent powders, liquid detergents and dishwash bars, dishwashing liquid and bars, and scouring powders, dry mix detergents, floor cleaners, dishwash liquid, detergent, wash liquid, floor cleaner liquid, fabric conditioner, and copper and brass cleaning powder. Additionally, the company owns and operates seven wind turbine generators with a capacity of 5 MW in Tamil Nadu. The company also exports its products. Ultramarine & Pigments Limited was incorporated in 1960 and is based in Mumbai, India.
