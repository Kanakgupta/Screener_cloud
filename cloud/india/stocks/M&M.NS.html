Auto / EV + AI in Mobility

Mahindra & Mahindra is embedding AI across its EV and automotive portfolio. The BE/XEV electric SUV lineup (launched FY25) features AI-powered ADAS (lane assist, automatic emergency braking), connected car AI (Adrenox platform), and OTA software updates. M&M also uses AI in manufacturing (predictive maintenance at Chakan plant) and AI for farm equipment (autonomous tractor project).
