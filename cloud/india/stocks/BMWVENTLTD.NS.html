Company Overview

BMW Ventures Limited engages in the trading and distribution of long and flat steel products in Bihar, India. The company offers thermo mechanically treated bars and galvanized wires, and structura - rectangular, square, and circular steel hollow sections; flat steel products, including galvanized corrugated sheets, galvanized sheets, hot rolled sheets and coils, and cold rolled sheets and coils; agrico tools, such as hoes, shovels, sickles, crowbars, pickaxes, and hammers. It also distributes John Deer tractor engines and spare parts; manufactures and sells polyvinyl chlorid pipes under BMW Polytube brand label; and rents construction equipment, as well as fabricates pre-engineered building and steel girders, and provides roll forming for galvanized plain sheets. The company was incorporated in 1994 and is based in Patna, India.
