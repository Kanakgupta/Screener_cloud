Company Overview

Universus Photo Imagings Limited manufactures, trades, and sells photographic and other imaging products. The company also manufactures, sells, distributes, converts, and produces X-ray films; and NTR films are used for photo albums, certificates, playing cards, gift cards, visiting cards, calendars, menu cards, wedding invitations, and a modelling portfolio. The company was formerly known as Jindal Photo Imaging Limited and changed its name to Universus Photo Imagings Limited in December 2019. Universus Photo Imagings Limited was incorporated in 2011 and is based in Gurugram, India.
