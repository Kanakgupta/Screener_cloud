Company Overview

Quintegra Solutions Limited provides information technology (IT) and consulting services in India. The company offers application management, such as development, maintenance, migration, and reengineering; product engineering; and SAP services, including SAP implementation services, SAP upgrades, SAP functional support, SAP production support, basis and technical consulting, and SAP application innovation. It also provides testing and validation; and technology consulting services. In addition, the company offers education and training, patient care, hospital management, and non-life insurance solutions. It serves banking and financial, healthcare, education and training, engineering, logistics, and telecom industries. The company was incorporated in 1994 and is headquartered in Chennai, India.
