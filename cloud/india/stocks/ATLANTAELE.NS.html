Company Overview

Atlanta Electricals Limited manufactures and sells transformers to institutional and corporate customers in India. The company's products include power and auto, furnace, inverter duty, green, and special duty transformers. It also provides after-sales support services. The company serves transmission, steel, dairies, solar, textile, construction, and infrastructure industries. The company serves various industries, such as transmission, steel, dairies, solar, textile, construction, infrastructure, etc. Atlanta Electricals Limited was founded in 1983 and is based in Anand, India.
