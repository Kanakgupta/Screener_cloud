Company Overview

Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Limited operates as a tech-enabled insurance distribution platform that connects customers, insurance advisors and insurers in India. The company offers products, such as health, car, bike, retirement, and life insurance, as well as mutual funds, loans, and credit cards. It also develops Insurance Hub, a cloud-based platform that provides a suite of APIs offering access to a broad catalogue of insurance products, KYC services, and a transformation and rules engine; Integration Studio, a developer-centric environment that supports the entire lifecycle of API integrations; Ninja SalesPro app, a tool designed for relationship managers; Turtlemint Academy, a digital learning and development platform designed to enhance the knowledge, skills, and productivity; TurtlemintPro, a mobile application for financial advisors to sell insurance and financial products, with access to marketing tools, training, and support; Turtlefin, an embedded insurance and SaaS solutions for banks, NBFCs, insurers, and e-commerce platforms; and Turtlemint Consumer App, an application for customers to manage their policies, track benefits, and handle claims. The company was incorporated in 2015 and is based in Mumbai, India.
