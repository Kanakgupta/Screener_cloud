AI Exposure AI AI Infrastructure / Servers
Core Focus

Netweb Technologies is India's largest AI-server / HPC system builder. Offers Tyrone AI servers (NVIDIA H100/H200/B200 based), supercomputers, and AI cloud infrastructure. Key supplier for IIT-Madras, ISRO, defence and several Indian AI cloud startups.

Key Initiatives
  • AI supercomputing & HPC systems Netweb designs and builds indigenous high-performance computing and AI servers (Tyrone) — directly supplying GPU clusters for India's AI and supercomputing demand.
  • AI systems for enterprise & government Private AI cloud, storage and HPC solutions for data centres, research labs and government under Make-in-India sourcing.
  • Full-stack AI infrastructure From AI servers to liquid cooling and software, Netweb offers a domestic alternative for AI-infrastructure build-outs.
Why Investors Should Care
  • One of the few listed Indian pure-plays on AI-server and supercomputing hardware.
  • Direct beneficiary of India's sovereign-AI and data-centre capex wave.
  • Make-in-India sourcing preferences support a strong order pipeline.
  • Small scale and hardware-margin pressure make execution and premium valuation key risks.
