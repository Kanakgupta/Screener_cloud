Company Overview

GLASS WALL SYSTEMS (INDIA) PVT. LTD. provides turnkey architectural facade solutions, including the design, manufacturing, and installation of building facades. The company offers glazing systems and custom-designed architectural metal works, managing projects with in-house design, engineering, and project management teams. It utilizes advanced design and production software, dedicated production lines for unitized systems, doors, and windows, and operates manufacturing plants with in-house powder coating and sheet fabrication units. The company conducts skill training programs and workshops for designers and engineers. It serves clients in the construction and real estate development industries, including those involved in modern architectural projects and building facades, as well as clients for global flagship projects in regions such as India, USA, Australia, Qatar, and Sri Lanka. The company was founded in 2010 and is based in Mumbai, India.
