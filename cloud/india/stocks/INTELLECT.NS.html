Company Overview

Intellect Design Arena Limited engages in the development, marketing, and sale of integrated banking software systems in India and internationally. It is also involved in supporting the implementation of the systems; and provides help desk support services to users of intellect software systems. In addition, the company provides software development services in the areas of wholesale banking comprising corporate core, commercial loan, payments, liquidity management, investments, virtual accounts, trade and supply chain finance, receivables and collections, treasury, corporate escrow deposits, and corporate banking exchange. Further, it offers consumer banking, including core banking, lending, cards, collection management, and digital engagement; wealth management; financial advisory, such as brokerage, custody, and transfer agency; insurance, such as magic submission, risk analyst, and underwriting; and bank treasury. The company develops IntellectAI's Purple Fabric an open business impact AI platform that delivers secure, decision-grade intelligence with measurable business impact; eMACH.ai, an composable and open finance platform which focuses on finance operations; iTurmeric, an integration and configuration platform; and Academic TeamSpace, an AI collaboration platform for the education sector. It serves retail and corporate customers, central banks, wealth managers, private bankers, card issuers, as well as capital market participants such as brokers, custodians, asset managers, insurance carriers, government enterprises, corporates and retail chains, and educational institutions. Intellect Design Arena Limited was incorporated in 2011 and is headquartered in Chennai, India.
