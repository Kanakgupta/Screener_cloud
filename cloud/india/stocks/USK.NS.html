Company Overview

Udayshivakumar Infra Limited engages in the execution of infrastructure projects primarily in India. The company is involved in the construction of roads; toll collection on highways; and other related government contracts. It also provides ready mix concrete. Udayshivakumar Infra Limited was incorporated in 1995 and is based in Davanagere, India.
