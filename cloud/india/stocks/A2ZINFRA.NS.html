Company Overview

A2Z Infra Engineering Limited offers engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) and facility management services in India and internationally. The company operates through the Engineering Services (ES), Facility Management Services, Municipal Solid Waste Management, and Others segments. It provides EPC solutions, including distribution, transmissions, substation, feeder renovation, rural and railway electrification, IT application, renewable energy generation, and telecom EPC services; facility management services comprising technical, front office management, vendor management, and facilities management consulting services; facility management home services, such as professional pest control, upholstery shampooing, electric professionals, AC service and repair, plumbing professionals, professional carpenters, honing and polishing of floor, and painter services; and municipal solid waste management services. The company also develops the Ecotech green and Bus Shelter-cum-green toilet; bricks made from municipal solid wastes under the Magic Brick name; and a bicycle under the E-Hiran name. In addition, it engages in renewable energy generation. The company was formerly known as A2Z Maintenance & Engineering Services Limited and changed its name to A2Z Infra Engineering Limited in October 2014. A2Z Infra Engineering Limited was incorporated in 2002 and is based in Gurugram, India.
