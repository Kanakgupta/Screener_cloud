Company Overview

Peninsula Land Limited, through its subsidiaries, engages in the real estate development activities in India. The company develops projects, such as retail ventures, commercial, and residential complexes. It also rents properties. The company was formerly known as Morarjee Realties Limited and changed its name to Peninsula Land Limited in April 2006. Peninsula Land Limited was incorporated in 1871 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
