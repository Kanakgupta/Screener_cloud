Company Overview

Caliber Mining and Logistics Limited provides coal mining services in India. The company offers rake loading, overburden removal, coal extraction and logistics, coal loading and unloading, and road transportation and coordination of rail transportation services, as well as engages in coal trading activities. Caliber Mining and Logistics Limited was incorporated in 2014 and is based in based in Nagpur, India.
