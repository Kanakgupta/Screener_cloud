Company Overview

Investment & Precision Castings Limited, together with its subsidiary, engages in the production of investment castings in India and internationally. The company operates through two segments, Investment Castings and Power Generation. It offers transmission parts, such as gear shifter levers, forks, fingers, etc.; engine parts, including injector clamping brackets and pre-combustion chambers; clutch parts; and coolant connections, diffusers, gas boxes, gas tanks, etc., for the automotive industry. The company also provides gimbal boxes, pedestal units, and structural castings for aircraft and satellite launch vehicles; turbochargers, such as radial and axial nozzle rings; steam turbine blades, impellers, and diffusers for power plants; impellers, diffusers, and valve bodies for pumps and valves; knee joint and prosthetic assembly parts for medical implants; casting products for electrical and instrumentation; pneumatic, and frame and body casting products; and general engineering parts. In addition, it offers artisan castings that include sacral sculptures, statues, candlestands, lamps, etc., and barstock machined components from various materials consisting of steel, aluminum, nickel, copper, and cobalt-based alloys. Further, the company engages in the operation of wind turbine generators. It serves automotive, agricultural machinery, construction equipment, electric power generation equipment, food and dairy equipment, hand tools, machine tools, printing machinery, packaging machinery, pneumatic/hydraulic equipment, paper processing machinery, mining equipment, railways, textile machinery, pumps and valves, electrical switchgear, and oil field equipment industries. Investment & Precision Castings Limited was incorporated in 1975 and is based in Bhavnagar, India.
