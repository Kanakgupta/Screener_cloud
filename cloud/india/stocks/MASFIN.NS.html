Company Overview

MAS Financial Services Limited, a non-banking finance company, provides retail financing services in India. The company offers micro-enterprises, small and medium businesses, two-wheeler, commercial and used vehicles, salaried personal, tractor, industrial shed, machinery, and housing loans, as well as loans against property. It serves through a distribution network of partner microfinance institutions, non-banking finance companies, housing finance companies, and franchisees. MAS Financial Services Limited was incorporated in 1995 and is based in Ahmedabad, India.
