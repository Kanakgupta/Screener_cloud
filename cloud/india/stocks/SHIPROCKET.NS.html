Company Overview

Shiprocket Limited provides e-commerce enablement platform solutions in India. It offers Shiprocket Shipping, an e-commerce shipping platform that automates workflows across sales channels; ShiprocketX, an end-to-end cross border solution; Shiprocket Fulfillment, a warehousing, and packaging and shipping solution; Shiprocket Engage 360, a marketing platform; and Shiprocket Capital, a revenue-based financing platform. The company also provides Shiprocket Packaging for packaging solutions; Shiprocket Checkout, a platform that enables a one-click checkout for e-commerce websites; Shiprocket Cargo, a platform that offers merchants with access to heavy logistics providers, which enable merchants to make deliveries and track deliveries in real time; Shiprocket Omuni, an unified commerce platform that connects online marketplaces, brand websites, and physical retail stores into a single system; Shiprocket Promise for shopping; Shiprocket Amplify, an influencer marketing solution; Shiprocket Quick, a delivery solution; Delivery Boost; Shiprocket Sense for address and risk intelligence to minimise address inaccuracies and delivery failures; and Shunya AI, an AI solution for businesses. It serves small businesses, retail brands, direct-to-consumer merchants, and social commerce retailers. Shiprocket Limited was incorporated in 2011 and is based in Gurugram, India.
