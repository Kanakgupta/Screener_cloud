Company Overview

UFO Moviez India Limited, together with its subsidiaries, provides digital cinema distribution and in-cinema advertising services in India and the Middle East. The company offers caravan talkies, a traditional media tool for rural targeting; digital cinema system; impact ticketing platform; nova experience an end-to-end service provider for the exhibition sector; and UFO Framez, an in-cinema advertising that helps advertisers in local markets to connect with direct sales associates to reach the target audience through UFO moviez. It also provides UFO-M4, a satellite-based, e-cinema movie delivery platform; digital mastering, and movie marketing and release of films services; and operates club cinemas. In addition, the company engages in the sale and rental of digital cinema equipment and consumables. UFO Moviez India Limited was incorporated in 2004 and is based in Mumbai, India.
