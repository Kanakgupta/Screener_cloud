Company Overview

Innovision Limited provides manpower services, toll plaza management, and skill development training in India. It offers manned private security services, integrated facility management services, manpower sourcing and payrolling services; fee collection and other related services at toll plazas; and skill development training as a training partner for various central and state government schemes, as well as drone flying training services. The company was incorporated in 2007 and is based in Gurugram, India.
