Company Overview

Zee Media Corporation Limited publishes and broadcasts satellite television channels in India and internationally. It sells television programs and contents; and provides advertisement and subscription services. The company serves through news channels in various languages, including global English news channel under the WION name; regional news channels, such as Zee 24 Taas, Zee Punjab Haryana Himachal, Zee Madhya Pradesh Chattisgarh, Zee Rajasthan, Zee Bihar Jharkhand, Zee 24 ghanta, Zee 24 Kalak, Zee Uttar Pradesh Uttarakhand, Zee Delhi NCR Haryana, Zee Kannada News, and Zee Telugu News; digital only news channel, such as Zee Tamil News, and Zee Malayalam News; and national news channels comprising Zee News, Salaam Tv, Zee Bharat, and Zee Business. It also serves through websites, social media pages, and apps in a variety of languages. Zee Media Corporation Limited was incorporated in 1999 and is based in Noida, India.
