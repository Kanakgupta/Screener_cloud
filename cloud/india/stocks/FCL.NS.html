Company Overview

Fineotex Chemical Limited manufactures and sells textile chemicals, auxiliaries, and specialty chemicals in India and United States. The company provides textile chemicals for pre-treatment, dyeing, printing, and finishing processes, as well as enzymes; water treatment chemicals; and oilfield chemicals, such as drilling fluids, cementing fluids, production chemicals, and fracturing solutions. It also offers cleaning and hygiene products, including disinfection, housekeeping, kitchen and personal care, and laundry products. The company's products are used in textile, home care, hygiene, mining, garment, water treatment, leather, construction, paint, agrochemicals, and adhesives sectors. It also exports its products. The company was founded in 1979 and is based in Mumbai, India.
