Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited M&MFIN NIFTY_MIDNSEFINANCIAL

Financial Services · Credit Services · India
https://www.mahindrafinance.com

Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited, a non-banking financial company, engages in financing business in India. The company operates through Financing Activities and Other Reconciling Items segments. It offers vehicle financing for auto, utility vehicles, passenger cars, tractors, commercial vehicles, and construction equipment; pre-owned vehicle financing; and vehicle leasing solutions through its digital platform Quiklyz to fleet operators, farmers, businesses, self-employed, and salaried individuals. The company also provides loan against property, equipment finance, working capital finance, and supply chain finance for micro, small and medium enterprises (MSME); asset management and trusteeship services; insurance broking and advisory services; and engages in the life, health, motor, and property insurance distribution. It serves retail and commercial customers, individuals, and entrepreneurs, as well as businesses operating in agriculture, transportation, and small and medium enterprises. Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India. Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited is a subsidiary of Mahindra & Mahindra Limited.
