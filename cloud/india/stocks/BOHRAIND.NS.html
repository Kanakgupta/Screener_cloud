Company Overview

Bohra Industries Limited manufactures and trades in phosphate fertilizers in India. The company offers single super phosphate, granulated single super phosphate, triple super phosphate, rock phosphate, and other fertilizers. It markets its products under the MAHALAXMI brand. The company also offers its products through a network of dealers. The company was incorporated in 1996 and is based in Udaipur, India.
