Company Overview

Bharat Road Network Limited owns, designs, develops, builds, operates, and transfers road and related services in India. It is also involved in the project development and implementation; tolling operation and maintenance; highway management and development; claim and contracts management; and advisory and project management services, including construction supervision/debt syndication. The company was incorporated in 2006 and is based in Kolkata, India.
