Vaibhav Global Limited VAIBHAVGBL NSE

Consumer Cyclical · Luxury Goods · India
https://www.vaibhavglobal.com

Vaibhav Global Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacture and export of fashion jewelry and lifestyle products in India, the United States of America, the United Kingdom, Germany, and internationally. The company offers gemstones and accessories, and lifestyle products, such as home décor, beauty care, and apparels and accessories. It also provides call center and studio services. The company sells its products through 24/7 TV home-shopping channels, e-commerce websites, mobile apps, smart TV, OTT platforms, social media platforms, influence marketing, third-party marketplaces, and other digital means. The company was founded in 1980 and is headquartered in Jaipur, India. Vaibhav Global Limited is a subsidiary of Brett Enterprises Private Limited.
