Company Overview

Sadhana Nitro Chem Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of chemical intermediates and organic and performance chemicals in India and internationally. The company offers downstream derivatives of nitrobenzene and other intermediates for various applications in aerospace, pharma and agro, optical brightening agents, plastic additives, special fibers, epoxy resin hardeners, dyes, and performance chemicals. It also provides meta, ortho, and para amino phenol; metanilic acid; aniline; 1,2,3,4 butane tetra carboxylic acid; 2-anilino-6-dibutylamino-3-methylfluoran; 4,4 diamino diphenyl ether; aniline 2,5 disulphonic acid; aniline; developer pf20x; ammonium sulphate; n,n diethyl meta amino phenol; sodium meta nitrobenzene sulphonate; and dibutyl and diethyl keto acid. In addition, the company manufactures wireless network equipment and services. Sadhana Nitro Chem Limited was incorporated in 1973 and is based in Mumbai, India. Sadhana Nitro Chem Limited is a subsidiary of Manekchand Panachand Trading Investment Company Pvt Ltd.
