Company Overview

Oswal Agro Mills Limited engages in the development and trading of real estate properties in India. It operates through Trading, Real Estate, and Investment segments. It also trades in goods and commodities. In addition, the company invests in equity instruments and other securities; and surplus funds in inter-corporate deposits, mutual funds, and other miscellaneous incomes. Oswal Agro Mills Limited was incorporated in 1979 and is headquartered in New Delhi, India.
