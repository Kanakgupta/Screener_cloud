Plastiblends India Limited PLASTIBLEN NSEINFRA

Basic Materials · Specialty Chemicals · India
https://www.plastiblends.com

Plastiblends India Limited engages in the manufacture and sale of masterbatches in India and internationally. The company offers additive, white, black, color, polywhite (filler), PET, and white masterbatches; and conductive and engineered plastic compounds. Its products are used in agriculture, appliances, automotive and transportation, electrical and electronics, telecommunication, flexible packaging, healthcare and pharmaceutical, rigid packaging, textiles, and wire and cable industries. Plastiblends India Limited was incorporated in 1991 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
