Company Overview

Laser Power & Infra Limited manufactures and distributes power cables, conductors, and other components for the power transmission and distribution industry in India and internationally. The company operates in 2 segments, manufacturing and EPC (engineering, procurement, and construction). The company's products include galvanized steel earth wire, aluminum alloy wire rods, MV aerial bunched cables, PVC insulated copper cables, conductors, underground jelly-filled quad cables, railway power cables, and railway signaling cables. The company was incorporated in 1988 and is based in Kolkata, India.
