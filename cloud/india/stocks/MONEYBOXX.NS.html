Company Overview

Moneyboxx Finance Limited, a non-banking financial company, engages in the provision of financing services in India. The company offers small-ticket business loans under the Moneyboxx Vyapaar name; and mortgage loans under the Saral name to micro and small enterprises. It also operates Sikka App, an application that enables customers to apply and track loan applications, and manage EMIs, as well as invest in gold and health insurance products. The company was formerly known as Dhanuka Commercial Limited and changed its name to Moneyboxx Finance Limited in January 2019. Moneyboxx Finance Limited was incorporated in 1994 and is headquartered in Mumbai, India.
