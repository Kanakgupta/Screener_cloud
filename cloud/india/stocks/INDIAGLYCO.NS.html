Company Overview

India Glycols Limited manufactures and sells bio-based specialities and performance chemicals in India and internationally. It operates through Bio-based Specialities and Performance Chemicals, Potable Spirits, Ennature Biopharma, and Bio-Fuel segments. The company offers bio mono ethylene, di ethylene, and tri ethylene glycols; ethylene and diethylene glycol monoethyl ethers, ethylene and diethylene glycol monoethyl ether acetates, and brake fluids; biopolymers from agricultural guar gum, tamarind gum, and cassia gum; biofuels, which include ethanol, DDGS, and DWGS; specialty chemicals, such as plasticizers, oil fields chemicals, bioamines, methyl soyate, methyl diethanolamine (MDEA) and MDEA derivatives, bio-potash granules, and C-Smart specialty products; molasses-based and grain-based extra neutral alcohol; and specialty gases, including industrial and medical oxygen, food grade and industrial carbon dioxide, liquid argon and nitrogen, ethylene oxide, and bio ethylene oxide. It also provides Indian made foreign liquor, such as whiskey under Soulmate Blu and Single Reserve brands; vodka under the Amazing Vodka brand; rum under Zumba Lemoni Citrus, Zumba Black Spiced, and Beach House XXX brands; economy spirits under Bunty brand; and branded country liquor. In addition, the company offers active pharmaceutical ingredients, branded nutraceutical ingredients, standardized botanical extracts, and nicotine derivatives. It serves the pharma and healthcare; textiles; automotive; personal care; industrial and manufacturing; paints and coatings; oil and gas; food ingredients; packaging; potable spirits; and other various industries, as well as corporates, medium and small enterprises, traders, and distributors. The company exports its products. The company was formerly known as UP Glycols Limited and changed its name to India Glycols Limited in August 1986. India Glycols Limited was incorporated in 1983 and is headquartered in Noida, India.
