Company Overview

Elecon Engineering Company Limited manufactures and sells industrial gears and material handling equipment in India and internationally. The company operates in two segments, Material Handling Equipment and Transmission Equipment. It offers helical and bevel helical, planetary, worm, wind turbine, marine, and custom-built gear boxes; and customized gear boxes, loose gears, and couplings. The company also provides material handling equipment, such as raw material handling systems, stackers, scrapers/reclaimers, bagging and weighing machines, wagon and truck loaders, crushers, wagon tipplers and associated equipment, feeders, idlers and pulleys, magnates/weighers/detectors, port equipment, and cable reeling drums; and alternate energy products. In addition, it engages in the ferrous and non-ferrous foundry business. Further, the company offers gearbox repair and refurbish services; and material handling equipment support services. It serves cement, sugar, defense, steel, mineral, metal, mining, power, plastic, material handling, chemical, crane, paper, rubber, marine, and fertilizer industries, as well as windmills. Elecon Engineering Company Limited was founded in 1951 and is based in Vallabh Vidya Nagar, India.
