Company Overview

Pioneer Investcorp Limited provides investment banking and advisory services in India and internationally. It offers a range of investment banking services, including merchant banking, corporate finance advisory and strategic alliance, debt syndication, structured transaction, and merger and acquisition advisory services, as well as private equity advisory services comprising venture and growth capital, structured finance, buyout/control transaction, and situation finance services. The company also provides institutional securities trading services for government securities, state development loans, non-SLR bonds, corporate bonds, and money market instruments; portfolio management services; and insurance and reinsurance advisory services, such as direct and retail insurance, as well as reinsurance. Pioneer Investcorp Limited was incorporated in 1984 and is based in Mumbai, India.
