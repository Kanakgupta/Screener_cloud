Company Overview

Golden Tobacco Limited engages in the manufacturing, purchasing, processing, selling, and marketing tobacco and tobacco related products in India. The company offers cigarettes, slim/super slim cigarettes, cigars, cigarillos products, tobacco, and packed type cigarettes. It sells its products under the Panama, Chancellor, Golden's Gold Flake, Taj Chhap, Style, Esquire, Flair, June, and Just Black brand names. The company also exports its products to the United States, Europe, Russia, Singapore, Cambodia, Japan, the Middle East, and internationally. Golden Tobacco Limited was formerly known as GTC Industries Ltd. and changed its name to Golden Tobacco Limited in July 2008. Golden Tobacco Limited was founded in 1930 and is based in Vadodara, India.
