Company Overview

Embassy Developments Limited operates as a real estate company in India. The company project portfolio is real estate developers, specializing in the construction and development of residential, commercial, and Special Economic Zone (SEZ) projects. The company was formerly known as Equinox India Developments Limited and changed its name to Embassy Developments Limited in February 2025. Embassy Developments Limited was incorporated in 2006 and is based in Mumbai, India.
