Company Overview

Tasty Bite Eatables Limited manufactures and sells prepared food in India and internationally. The company offers ready-to-eat and ready-to-cook meals, sauces, frozen food, and culinary accompaniments for retail and foodservice customers. It also provides all-natural and organic entrees; organic rice; tomato-based sauces, gravy, curry, pastes, savory fillings, specialty sauces, and emulsion sauces; frozen-formed products, such as mixed vegetable, potato, paneer, cheese, plant-protein, and lentil burger patties, as well as plant-protein, mixed vegetable, potato, paneer, and cheese appetizers and finger food; and pasta and combo meals. In addition, the company offers snack products consisting of seasoned mozzarella sticks, paneer double deckers, cheesy jalapeno bites, cheese chili bursts, crispy onion rings, and veggie and cheese chili patties, as well as protein bowls, pizza sauces and toppings, Bombay aloo vada, and dal makhni products. It sells its products under the Cheffin' and Tasty Bite XCLUSIV brands. The company operates across North America, Europe, Asia, and Oceania. It exports its products. The company was incorporated in 1985 and is headquartered in Pune, India. Tasty Bite Eatables Limited operates as a subsidiary of Preferred Brands Foods (India) Private Limited.
