Company Overview

M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited, a retail-focused jewellery company, manufactures, markets, and sells jewellery products primarily in India. It offers cubic zirconia, diamond, and gold rings and necklaces; diamond and gold earrings, bangles, and pendants; gold chains; diamond mangalasutras; and pink tag products. The company also markets its products under the Pavitra, Rivaaz, Nayaab, RR Classics, CharmChime, Sitaare, NAQSH, Petite, Sunhere, and Ra Ra Ru names. It sells its products through retail stores, as well as through online. M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited was founded in 1999 and is based in Mumbai, India.
