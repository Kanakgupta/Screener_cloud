BCPL Railway Infrastructure Limited BCPL NSEINFRA

Industrials · Infrastructure Operations · India
https://www.bcril.com

BCPL Railway Infrastructure Limited, together with its subsidiary, executes railway electrification projects in India. It operates through Railway Overhead Electrification, Merchant Exports, and Edible Oils segments. The company is also involved in power transmission line activities; and extracts crude rice bran oil. It serves Indian railways and non-government parties. The company was incorporated in 1995 and is based in Kolkata, India.
