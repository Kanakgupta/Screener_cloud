Company Overview

Ddev Plastiks Industries Limited manufactures and sells customized compounds in India and internationally. It offers antifab, filled compounds, and master batches used in woven sacks, cement bags, and industrial packaging solutions; PVC compounds used in wire and cable, construction, and footwear industries; halogen free flame retardant (HFFR) compounds; and sioplas, XLPE compounds, semicons, and EHV compounds. The company also offers engineering plastic compounds used in the white goods, fast moving electrical goods sectors; anti-fibrillation compound; PVC compounds for electrical wiring and automotive sector; and polyethylene. It also exports its products. The company was founded in 1977 and is headquartered in Kolkata, India. Ddev Plastiks Industries Limited operates as a subsidiary of Bbigplas Poly Private Limited.
