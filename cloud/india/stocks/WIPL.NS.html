Company Overview

Western India Plywoods Limited manufactures and sells hardboard, plywood, and compreg wood products in India and internationally. The company offers fire retardant, resin surfaced shuttering, marine, and boiling water-resistant plywood; hard boards, such as standard hardboard, oil tempered, and perforated; densified woods, including compreg slats, moulded seats, WIPCHECK, WIPBEAR, WIPROC, WIP filter plates, WIPCOM, WIPLAM, and WIPWOOD; softwoods; furniture; doors; and WIPRESS, a hard and rigid board. It also provides value-added products. It serves customers automobiles customers, and packaging industry and construction companies. The Company was incorporated in 1945 and is based in Kannur, India.
