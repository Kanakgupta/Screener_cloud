Company Overview

Sanginita Chemicals Limited manufactures and sells cuprous chloride, cupric chloride, and copper sulphate in India and internationally. It also offers copper phthalocyanine blue. The company's products are used in dyes and pigments, paint, pharmaceuticals, electroplating, metal extraction and ink, carbon paper, and PVC pipe coating industries. It also exports its products. The company was founded in 2003 and is based in Gandhinagar, India. As of June 23, 2026, the Sanginita Chemicals Limited operates as subsidiary of B N G Investment LLC.
