Company Overview

KD Green Industries Limited engages in the manufacture, import, export, and trading of iron and steel products in India. The company offers GFRP rebar and byproducts; and eco-friendly building materials, such as AAC blocks, cement, and related products. It also provides various food and beverages, including snacks, dairy products, bakery items, canned and packed food, squashes, aerated and mineral water, syrups, soft and fruit drinks, and milk and milk products, as well as whole grains and pulses, rice, spices, organic, spice mixes and blends, freeze dried, and curry powders/mixtures, etc. The company was formerly known as Manbro Industries Limited and changed its name to KD Green Industries Limited in April 2026. Manbro Industries Limited was incorporated in 1992 and is based in Guwahati, India.
