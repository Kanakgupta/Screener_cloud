Data & method

USA: Zillow Home Value Index (typical mid-tier home value, monthly) for the 3,000 largest cities and every state. India: NHB RESIDEX — the National Housing Bank's official HPI@Assessment Prices and carpet-area prices (₹/sq ft) for 50 cities, with the long-run under-construction index for trend and seasonality. Tier 1/2/3 cities (population from GeoNames) without a city index show their state's official trend, clearly marked, and never an invented price. Signals combine valuation vs long-run trend, momentum and a damped-trend forecast with seasonality; the best price window is the low point of the projected path.

Map of India: drawn only from the official boundary of India as published by the Survey of India (via DataMeet), including the whole of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh; no international basemap is used for India. ★ marks every state and union-territory capital.

Housing forecasts are statistical projections with wide uncertainty; interest rates, supply and micro-location can dominate. Not real-estate, mortgage or investment advice.
